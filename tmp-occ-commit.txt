feat(render): white outline for units behind buildings (v0.1.133)

"Units behind buildings should show a white outline instead of being fully
visible or fully invisible." A unit painted over by a building in the
back-to-front (cellX+cellY) isometric draw pass used to vanish; now it gets
a white silhouette of its own role shape (owner-tint edge, no ground
shadow) stamped on a layer above the buildings — the classic AoE2 cue.

- occlusionSilhouettes.ts (NEW, pure): depthKey(e)=e.x+e.y SHARED with the
  render sort (a test asserts they agree, so the cue can't desync from what
  paints over); buildingSilhouettePolygon (the 6-pt hexagon a building
  actually paints — footprint diamond + wall height + pitched-roof ridge,
  construction 0.4x, memory ghosts excluded); pointInPolygon; and
  computeOccludedUnits (a unit is occluded iff some building with a greater
  depth key covers the unit's screen centre; AABB pre-reject, O(units x
  buildings), sub-ms at RTS scale).
- sceneRenderer.ts: a new occlusionOutlineLayer between fog and health bars
  (z = creation order, no setDepth), cleared each frame; after the entity
  loop the occluded units are stamped via a new silhouette mode on
  unitRenderer.drawUnit; the draw sort now shares depthKey.
- getOccludedUnitStates() exposed through GameScene + browserTestApi.

Design was scoped by an ultracode 3-agent fan-out (render layers /
detection geometry / outline + edges). Ultracode adversarial review then
caught a MEDIUM pre-commit: the pitched-roof silhouette used a full-height
apex over the back corner, but drawIsoBuilding paints a HIP roof whose ridge
is inset toward centre — a ~37px over-claim band that stamped false ghosts
on units poking above their own roofline. Fixed by deriving the top point
from hipRoofFaces (the same geometry drawIsoBuilding paints), with a
regression test. Verified on the real default view: two villagers behind
the Town Center render as white ghosts on its roof. Tests:
occlusionSilhouettes (12), a unitRenderer silhouette test, a browser test.
Render-only:
simulation, hit-testing, health bars, selection, fog, saves, replays
unchanged. Four gates + browser suite green. Spec 14.5, changelog,
drift-log, devlog. 0.1.132 -> 0.1.133.
