feat(render): pitched building roofs — buildings read as buildings (v0.1.132)

"The building looks too abstract." The extruded volume already had wall
faces, doors, windows, roof tiles and per-role accents — what was missing
was a ROOF SHAPE. House-like buildings (town-center, house, mill,
drop-site, military/barracks, blacksmith, market, monastery) now cap their
volume with a pitched ridged-hip roof — two sloped side planes meeting at a
ridge, lit left / shadowed right — instead of a flat diamond top, the
dominant "abstract box" signal.

- isoBuilding.ts: hipRoofFaces (two pentagon planes + ridge), pitchedApexPx
  (footprint-scaled ridge height, clamped 11-38px), roleHasPitchedRoof
  (flat roles = fortress/tower/wall/wonder/farm), pitchedRoofDetailSegments
  (hips + courses that stay ON the roof eaves), and a `pitched` flag in
  drawIsoBuilding.
- buildingRoofAccents.ts: an anchorLift so the TC cupola / monastery cross /
  barracks banner / mill blades ride on the ridge, not mid-slope.
- buildingRenderer.ts: passes pitched + the matching ridge lift.

Flat-topped roles preserved: fortress/tower/wall keep their crenellated
battlement, the wonder its dome, the farm its plot. Roof keeps the owner
tint for team ID; lit/shadow slopes give form.

Adversarial review (3 dims) confirmed 2 MEDIUM, both fixed pre-commit:
(1) the hip/course detail lines were drawn to the GROUND corners, a
wall-height below the eaves, streaking across the walls — refactored into
the pure pitchedRoofDetailSegments (targets the roof eaves) with a
no-streak regression test; (2) added a renderer-level per-role
pitched/flat integration test. Verified on the real default view + the
building-showcase fixture. Render-only: footprints, hit-testing, health
bars, selection, construction, fog memory, simulation, saves, replays
unchanged. Four gates green. Spec 14.5, changelog, devlog. 0.1.131 -> 0.1.132.
