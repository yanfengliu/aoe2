# Behaviour matches the real Age of Empires II (2026-08-23, owner)

One of [this repo's local rules](../local-rules.md), where "above" and "below" mean earlier and later in its list.

The game must **function identically to the real Age of Empires II** — same costs, rates, counters, tech-tree gating, controls, and consequences. A divergence is a defect even when nothing in this repo asserts the correct value.

**Why:** owner directive 2026-08-23. It is the standing tiebreak for every "is this good enough?" question: the answer is whatever the real game does.

**How to apply:** resolve rules in the [§1 source-of-truth order](../../../design/spec-final.md) — `design/stats/` CSVs first, then the spec, then official references. **Definitive Edition is the authoritative edition** (owner, 2026-08-23: "HD" in the directive is shorthand for the real game, and the CSVs are DE-derived; their unit and technology costs and times are since 2026-09-23, when both files were re-sourced from the DE dataset, SiegeEngineers/aoe2techtree at `3bb43b14` (DE update 185872), pinned in each file's header; before that they were mostly Conquerors-era); where an HD-era rule differs from DE, DE wins and the choice is recorded. A remembered game rule is a hypothesis — verify it against an authoritative reference before implementing to match it; so is an LLM conformance probe's rule claim, which has misdiagnosed a correct implementation as a bug. One deliberate exception, owner-set and about LOOK rather than behaviour: all art is original/procedural rather than reproduced AoE2 assets. The modern HUD chrome was a second until 2026-09-25, when the owner put the HUD inside the DE target (the HUD entry above). The Moebius art style is a kept option, not a divergence from the target (spec §2.1, §14.5).

**First-party text on this machine (2026-10-15).** Definitive Edition is installed at `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\AoE2DE`. Its strings, `resources\en\strings\key-value\key-value-strings-utf8.txt`, are DE's own text: help and tooltip lines, map sizes with their player labels, resource names such as "Wonder Timer Modifier". Work 132's worker found it and sourced the countdown rule from it. Read it first when sourcing a rule, cite the line, and only read: nothing in this repo may copy DE's files or assets.

**Restated by the owner 2026-09-02, mid-play-test:** "The rendering needs to feel realistic. The UI needs to be elegant. Game needs to be well optimized and correct. Bug free." This does not change the north star above — realism is for the WORLD within the original voxel art direction, elegance for the HUD, whose target since 2026-09-25 is Definitive Edition's HUD rather than the "modern looking UI" of 2026-08-18 — but it fixes the order of proof: a change to either surface is judged by playing it, not only by a passing test, and the play test that found the placement-mode panel collapse is the template.
