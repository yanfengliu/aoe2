# The HUD is inside the Definitive Edition target (2026-09-25, owner), and there is one HUD (the coordinator's reading); replaces "HUD styling: modern, not medieval" (2026-08-18)

One of [this repo's local rules](../local-rules.md), where "above" and "below" mean earlier and later in its list.

Asked whether the Definitive Edition visual target covers the HUD, the owner answered: *"Yes include HUD"*. The coordinator's reading, recorded as the coordinator's and not as the owner's words: the HUD becomes Definitive-Edition-like, there is ONE HUD, and Moebius stays selectable as an art style for the game world only, so nothing in the HUD depends on the art style.

**Why:** the modern chrome — translucent dark-glass panels, one hairline border, soft two-part elevation, a token palette, generous radii — came from the owner asking for "a modern looking UI" on 2026-08-18, when the north star was "a perfect AoE2 HD clone", and it replaced the framed wood-and-stone chrome of v0.1.38. On 2026-09-23 the north star became Definitive Edition's look, with Moebius kept as an art-style option (spec §14.5). That decision did not mention the HUD, so the modern chrome stood and the question went to the owner as decision D3 of the de-look plan. The answer leaves one look divergence: all art stays original. DE's HUD art is copyrighted, so a DE-like HUD is original and procedural like the world.

**How to apply:** the HUD work is steps H1–H7 of `docs/threads/current/de-look/PLAN.md` (the chrome, the layout, portraits, the resource bar, type, the minimap, cursors and in-world selection marks), ranked there with the world steps. New HUD work aims at the DE target rather than extending the modern look. Until a step lands, what it replaces keeps shipping and keeps its gates; a fix to today's chrome is still a fix. What holds for any chrome: every HUD colour comes from the tokens in `styles.css:root` (`--hud-surface*`, `--hud-hairline*`, `--hud-text*`, `--hud-accent`, the per-resource hues, and the radius/elevation scales), no HUD rule gets a raw colour literal, and text holds 4.5:1 and labels 3:1 against the worst backdrop the panel shows (`tests/ui/hudPalette.test.ts`). That test's ban on the old wood-and-stone literals and its reduced-transparency check guard the modern chrome; H1 revises them, and nothing removes them before it. `hudChrome.css` owns the chassis, `hudCommandPanel.css` the components inside it, `hudIcons.css` glyph sizing.

Authoritative description: `design/spec-final.md` §14.1 "HUD chrome".
