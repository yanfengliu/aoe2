# Slice 10 — Real AI baseline

Date: 2026-04-17.
Roadmap: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-04-17-post-sheep-roadmap.md` (slice 10).

## Goal

Replace the current Barracks-rush stub (`prototypeAi`) with a planner-
style AI that plays a full RTS match across all four ages, making
decisions that depend on the game state rather than a scripted loop.

## Scope (v1 — pragmatic, not perfect)

- **Blackboard / planner interface:** a per-owner state object that
  tracks goals, pending actions, observed threats, and economy
  targets.
- **Opening plan:** scout the map, gather toward age-up, build House
  timing to avoid pop block.
- **Villager assignment:** dynamic rebalance across food / wood / gold
  / stone based on current needs (food for age-up + military,
  wood for buildings, gold / stone for later tiers).
- **Age-up decisions:** advance to Feudal / Castle / Imperial once the
  player has the required buildings and a rough resource buffer.
- **Military production:** pick a unit mix per age (Feudal: Spearman +
  Archer + Skirmisher; Castle: Pikeman + Crossbowman + Knight;
  Imperial: Halberdier + Arbalest + Cavalier or equivalent).
- **Attack-group formation:** accumulate N units before pushing, retreat
  if strength drops below threshold.
- **Scouting response:** if enemy units sighted near base, build a
  Watch Tower toward the incoming direction.
- **Difficulty modifiers:** a `DifficultyLevel` knob (e.g.,
  `'easy' | 'standard' | 'hard'`) tweaks AI gather-rate multiplier and
  decision interval.

## Explicit out of scope

- Monk conversion / heal AI usage.
- Relic collection AI.
- Multi-front coordinated attacks.
- Wonder or Relic-victory strategies.
- Allied AI cooperation.
- Civ-specific strategy tuning (the AI will not play Britons
  differently from another civ).

## Where in the code

- `src/game/simulation/createSimulationBridge.ts`
  - Existing `prototypeAi` system gets rewritten. It currently runs
    a scripted Barracks rush; we replace with a planner driven by a
    per-owner `aiState: Map<ownerId, AiState>` side map.
  - `AiState` shape:
    ```ts
    interface AiState {
      difficulty: DifficultyLevel;
      plan: 'opening' | 'feudal-push' | 'castle-push' | 'imperial-push' | 'defend' | 'retreat';
      villagerTargets: Partial<Record<ResourceKind, number>>;
      attackGroup: EntityRef[];
      lastDecisionTick: number;
      ...
    }
    ```
  - Decision loop runs every N ticks (N = decisionInterval based on
    difficulty) to reduce per-tick cost: scan state, update plan,
    issue commands.
  - Use existing `issueUnitMoveCommand`, `issueUnitAttackCommand`,
    `startConstruction`, `enqueueTraining`, `queueResearch` so the AI
    operates through the same seams the human player does.

- New `src/game/simulation/ai.ts` — home for helpers like
  `pickNextBuildTarget(owner, world)`,
  `pickUnitMix(age)`, `villagerRebalance(owner)`.

## Tests

New `tests/simulation/aiPlayer.test.ts`:

1. **AI ages up** — full deterministic match where AI advances through
   Feudal, Castle, Imperial within a fixed tick budget.
2. **AI assigns villagers to food / wood / gold** — after 300 ticks,
   owned villagers are distributed non-trivially across at least 3
   resource types.
3. **AI builds a Watch Tower toward enemy sighting** — fixture where
   an enemy scout wanders into AI vision; after X ticks, a Watch
   Tower foundation exists in a cell between the scout and the AI's
   Town Center.
4. **AI pushes with an attack group** — AI accumulates a group of
   at least 5 military units before moving toward enemy base.
5. **Difficulty modifies gather rate** — `'hard'` AI accumulates more
   resources per tick than `'easy'`. Vitest asserts a gap after a
   fixed tick count.
6. **Full match runs to outcome** — AI-vs-AI match on a small map
   produces a victory or defeat within the tick budget (no
   deadlock).

Browser: one case that boots the default scenario and asserts the
enemy AI has aged up within X seconds of play.

## Notes

- The AI is deterministic (no RNG use beyond what's already in
  simulation) so tests can assert exact states after N ticks.
- The decision interval is 30 ticks by default (3 seconds at TPS
  10) to keep per-tick cost low.
- Old `prototypeAi` was nested inside `createWorld`; the new one
  shares that home but calls out to `src/game/simulation/ai.ts`
  helpers.
