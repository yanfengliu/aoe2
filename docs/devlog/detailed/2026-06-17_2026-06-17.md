## 2026-06-17 — campaign-11 finding (c) AI never ages up despite sufficient food — FIXED via age-up cost reserve (v0.1.48) [thread ai-age-up-priority]

- **Timestamp:** 2026-06-17
- **Action:** fixed the deterministic AI's age-up stall (campaign-11 secondary finding c). Ground-truth `replay:inspect` of `output/playtests-llm/campaign-11.json` showed owner 2 reached **food=510 at tick 3000** with a complete **Barracks + Lumber Camp + Mill** (3 of 2 needed Feudal prereqs) + an **idle TC** (6 villagers = Dark-Age cap) yet stayed Dark Age all 6000 ticks, oscillating food 446–510 while massing Militia 10→19. A custom replay probe confirmed the prereqs were complete (not under construction) at tick 3000.
- **Root cause (engine-debug-tools-first; replay + probe, NOT the conformance probe — this finding came from ground-truth replay, it is NOT in `campaign-11.findings.md`):** in `aiSystem.ts` a Militia (Barracks, 60 food + 20 gold) is pushed BEFORE the feudal-age research (TC, 500 food) in one decision cycle. Both affordable individually at 510 food, not together (560 > 510); the FIFO handler spends the Militia first (510→450) and the age-up research then silently no-ops (best-effort spend). `savingForAgeUp` suppresses military only at 300–499 food, so the instant food crossed 500 a Militia dropped it back under — the AI never held 500 food through a decision→handler cycle. Military is the only pre-age-up spend that consumes the constrained FOOD — build placements are also sequenced before the age-up research but spend wood/stone, while villager / building-tech / monastery come after (FIFO protects the research from those), so only military needed the reserve.
- **Fix (no engine/save change):** RESERVE the next age-up's cost. `ageUpReserve = researchCost(nextAgeTech)` when the AI qualifies for the age (its `canAdvanceTo*Age` check passes), else `{}`; a `canAffordWithReserve(stockpile, cost, reserve)` helper requires the stockpile to cover the unit cost ON TOP of that reserve; the military block uses it instead of the bare `canAfford`. So military spends only from the surplus above the age-up cost. Both helpers (`ageUpReserveCost` / `canAffordWithReserve`) are pure functions extracted to `ai.ts` (keeps `aiSystem.ts` under its 788-LOC budget cap + gives them direct unit tests). **2 iterations:** iter-1 fully SUPPRESSED military while a fundable age-up was pending — green on the regression fixture but REGRESSED `aiPlayer.test.ts` "accumulates 5+ military before pushing in Feudal" on the rich `ai-planner-fixture` (2000-of-every-resource → perpetual fundable age-up → AI rushed ages, trained no military). iter-2's reserve fixes both: at 510 food the 500 reserve leaves 10 surplus (no Militia → age-up commits); at 2000 food it leaves ~1490 surplus (trains military AND ages up).
- **TDD:** new `ai-age-up-priority-fixture` (AI owner 2, Dark Age, 520 food, deep gold, no food income, complete TC + Barracks + Mill, 6 villagers) + an integration test asserting the AI reaches Feudal within 2500 ticks. RED before fix (`age=dark-age food=460 militia=1`), GREEN after. The integration test + 2 pure-helper tests live in a NEW `aiAgeUpPriority.test.ts` (split out so `aiPlayer.test.ts` stays under the 500-LOC cap). **Test update:** `aiPlayer.test.ts`'s "hard AI accumulates more food than easy AI" now measures PEAK Dark-Age food, not final stockpile — the fix makes the faster hard AI age up within the 1500-tick window (spending 500 food), so its FINAL food dips below the slower (not-yet-aged) easy AI; the old assertion relied on the very never-age-up bug being fixed. Full `aiPlayer.test.ts` re-run guards the rich-fixture age-up / 5+-military / barracks-rush / difficulty / monk / wonder assertions.
