### Reasoning

The visual oracle was shipped as a pure module in the closed `llm-agent-playtest` thread (Phase 4.A) but never wired into the runner. Phase-6.C.1 was originally just "build the dashboard" but turned out to require the upstream wiring as a precondition — captured in the diff as a single coherent change.

The `===` exact-match comparison (vs `<=`) is the right semantic: capturing at tickAfter when the checkpoint was somewhere earlier produces a misleading screenshot the oracle would compare against the wrong baseline. Skipping with a warn + surfacing as missingTicks is honest.

The HIGH-only gate aligns with how the deterministic playtest's other oracles work: severe regressions fail CI; informational signals surface in the summary without blocking. The 0.5% medium threshold catches sub-pixel jitter that's expected across CI environments (anti-aliasing, GPU vendor differences) and shouldn't fail the build; the 5% high threshold catches real visual regressions (missing sprites, broken HP bars, fog rendering glitches).

Reviewer disagreement on L1 (file-size budget on `playtest-llm.mjs`) was resolved by deferring: the file is over 500 LOC but isn't subject to the file-size-budget test (which checks `.ts`/`.tsx` in `src/` and `tests/` only). Splitting into helper modules is a sensible follow-up but doesn't add immediate value.
