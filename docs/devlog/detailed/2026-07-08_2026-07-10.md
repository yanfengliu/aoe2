## 2026-07-10 — Render perf: terrain-layer cache, forced re-render 3.64ms → 0.60ms (v0.1.131)

**Action:** Investigated a "the game is slow" report measurement-first (`docs/debugging/2026-07-10-render-perf-terrain-cache.md`).
