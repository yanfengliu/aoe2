## Smooth speed-matched voxel gait (v0.1.150)

**Outcome and ownership.** The experimental voxel renderer now derives each live unit's rigid locomotion pose from the same interpolated root path the player sees. Per-`id:generation` history advances a wrapped AoE role-stride phase only by displayed distance, so a faster root traverses the step cycle faster, two half-distance samples equal one full sample, and a stopped root cannot walk in place. Humanoid boots visibly reach and lift in opposition, legs/arms counter-swing, cavalry uses a longer paired stride, monks sway, and siege wheels retain distance phase. Root interpolation, simulation position, pathfinding, collision, commands, fog, selection, hit geometry, saves, and replay data are unchanged. The sibling `voxel` package received documentation only; City and Townscaper remain free to choose different motion clocks and continuity rules.

**Pause, corners, and ground correctness.** The first draft divided displacement by Phaser wall time. Adversarial review proved that a long pause then collapsed resume speed for an unselected scene while selection-forced redraws kept timestamps fresh, making identical worlds resume differently. `sceneRenderer` now injects simulation display time, `(tick + interpolationAlpha) * 1000 / TPS`; it freezes during manual/replay pause and rewinds across replay discontinuity, while the independent frame clock continues ambient breathing/tails/staffs. Final review also caught that `ReplayController.pause()` zeroed a nonzero interpolation alpha: the controller now preserves its accumulator and alpha, consumes zero delta on the first resumed frame, and retains the established immediate first tick only for genuinely fresh playback. Repeated same-time redraws preserve the complete gait sample. Moving headings ease along the shortest angular turn instead of snapping at AoE's X-then-Y path corners. A direction-aligned pitch matrix rotates flexion around the horizontal axis perpendicular to travel, and transformed-corner clearance lifts boots/horse legs just enough to avoid ground penetration for X, Z, and diagonal poses.

**Capacity and lifecycle.** `aoeVoxelResources.ts` now emits six stable lanes: static and animated matte, static and animated metal, contact shadow, and memory. This prevents a single ambient-active unit from subjecting every static tree/building/resource in that surface to the engine's 16,384-total-slot active-batch ceiling. Ambient animation is allocated by whole identity in canonical order up to the shared 8,192-active-slot budget; a multi-part identity at the cutoff degrades all-or-none to its deterministic static base pose. If a future producer accidentally attaches animation to shadow or memory geometry, the part stays visible in its static lane with animation stripped rather than silently disappearing. History remains bounded to current live units and resets on disappearance, memory projection, generation replacement, bridge replacement, or a rewound clock. A data-only browser diagnostic exposes copied gait history for one identity; no Three object or simulation mutator crosses the test seam.

No simulation state, command, selection, hit geometry, fog, health, save, or replay contract changed; attack/gather/projectile timing and skeletal clips remain deferred.

The Three backend rejects unsupported snapshots before accepted state mutates.
