# 2026-07-19 — narrow-choke queueing (v0.3.2)

- **Timestamp and request:** 2026-07-19; units entering a narrow space should wait behind one another instead of wrapping around the leader, with temporary unit traffic distinguished from permanent roadblocks.
- **Research:** AoE II update history and Ensemble's coordinated-movement material support a two-layer model: durable terrain/structures drive route search, while moving units are handled by local coordination. The source record and applied boundary are in `docs/threads/done/narrow-choke-queueing/RESEARCH.md`.
- **RED reproduction:** the real `narrow-corridor-fixture` placed three villagers abreast in one coarse corridor cell; the first contract failed with two entrants on tick 2. Successive adversarial passes produced REDs for task movers, bends, head-on flow, same-tick activation, misleading targets, longer cycles, co-located origins, stale intent, multi-occupancy, and owner isolation.
- **Action:** inserted `movementTrafficOps.ts` between permanent-only A* planning and fine stepping for commanded, monk, and villager-task movement. Straight and turning one-cell legs use an owner-scoped origin reservation and centered lane; acyclic followers wait with their route intact, while a fully occupied directed dependency loop releases only its lowest eligible id when every branch closes through a current or previous-tick caller.
- **Persistence:** `UnitTransformComponent` additively carries direction X/Y, intent key, and attempt tick; legacy state learns the fields on first use. Arbitration never reads the transient route cache. `movementPersistenceTypes.ts` holds the additive movement authority separately so the core `types.ts` catalog remains under the 500-LOC gate.
- **Review:** five adversarial passes closed four rounds of substantive findings; the final pass reported no code blocker. The complete synthesis is `docs/threads/done/narrow-choke-queueing/2026-07-19/1/REVIEW.md`.
- **Result:** 2,165 unit tests passed and 2 skipped across 287 passed and 1 skipped files; typecheck, lint, and the 565-module production build passed. `npm audit --omit=dev` and `npm audit` both reported zero vulnerabilities.
- **Boundary:** open-terrain subcell packing, autonomous scout/herdable movement, formation regrouping, persistent opposing-flow fairness, and idle-blocker timeouts are unchanged.

# 2026-07-19 — convincing tree forms (v0.3.3)

- **Request:** trees should have variety in color and form as part of a broader visual-conviction pass.
- **RED:** the prior recipe gave every tree the same four-block broadleaf topology and nearly the same greens. New cluster classification tests initially reported only broadleaf forms and one canopy palette: 18 passed, 2 failed.
- **Action:** the position-seeded recipe now chooses broadleaf, tall tiered conifer, or asymmetrical wind-swept geometry and one of three natural canopy palettes, then layers bounded within-family jitter. All forms retain the exact trunk/crown part-key set used by recipe silhouettes.
- **Visual evidence:** the fixed paused default-map capture changed 16,854 of 480,000 pixels (3.5113%), confined to the forest cluster and the translucent HUD pixels directly above it; the after frame visibly mixes tiered evergreens, low broad crowns, offset crowns, and dark/olive/blue-green foliage.
- **Focused result:** 74 rendering tests passed; the final variation plus file-size boundary was 23/23. Full gates passed 2,168 unit tests with 2 skipped across 287 passed and 1 skipped files, typecheck, zero-warning lint, and the 565-module production build.
- **Review:** independent adversarial review passed the recipe, hit-proxy, and file-size probes 60/60. It found one substantive documentation overclaim: independent position hashes cannot guarantee every small forest contains all three forms. The spec now states map-wide diversity honestly, and changelog wording now distinguishes synchronized recipe-driven picking from byte-identical hit polygons. Synthesis: `docs/threads/current/visual-conviction/2026-07-19/1/REVIEW.md`.

# 2026-07-19 — terrain materials and moving reflective water cues (v0.3.4)

- **Pinned boundary:** exact Voxel `faa00bf2ef83e5134bd9ca62c0b36cd0cfbe394b` supports standard-material roughness/metalness and bounded harmonic instance transforms, but no bitmap/normal/environment maps or planar reflection. Mixed terrain chunks therefore stay opaque; AoE layers low-profile details without changing the dependency pin.
- **RED:** the new focused terrain-detail contract began with 4 expected failures and 1 passing flat-terrain/picking invariant because the old recipe had only sparse matte glints, rocks, tufts, and logs.
