## The Siege Onager splashes, and attack-ground fires on every reload and only for units whose shot blasts (v0.3.236)

**Timestamp:** 2026-09-24 to 25 (worktree `siege-onager-blast`, begun on `1ea83922` and rebased three times: onto `6a7ad265`, the attack-warning lane's v0.3.235, and last onto `04fee601`, the owner decisions and the gate-lock rule)

**Action.** `isAreaProjectile` is every projectile attacker with a blast radius, read from the blast table; its own list, `['mangonel', 'onager']`, is gone. `canAttackGround` (a unit whose shot blasts where it lands, which is the mangonel line) gates the attack-ground validator, whose refusal now names the unit and its id, and the attack-ground step, which drops an order its unit cannot carry out. The step counts the reload down. The Siege Onager holds fire inside 3 tiles (units.csv: 3-8). New: `fixtures/blastCensus.ts` and its layout `fixtures/blastCensusLayout.ts`, one fixture per unit in the blast table, registered from the table; `tests/simulation/blastCensus.test.ts`; three cases in `attackGround.test.ts`; `tests/browser/attack-ground-hotkey.spec.ts`, the first browser spec to give the order through G and a click; and a Siege Onager scenario in `playerHitCensus.test.ts`, which landed on main with v0.3.235. Spec §10.3 and §10.7, the defect register (with the rollover of the 2026-09-23 stats-files entry, main having rolled the prices entry first), the changelog, and this file's rollover.

**Reproduced first, on a headless bridge.** The census, written before the fix, failed 5 of its 15 attack cases: the Siege Onager's attack on a unit landed by tick 10 and spared the witness 1.41 cells out, inside its 1.5 radius; on a House, by tick 14, the same; its ground order hurt nothing in 600 ticks; both demolition ships' ground orders were accepted. A throwaway probe (deleted) read the rest: after 300 ticks under a ground order the Demolition Ship was alive and still ordered, with every enemy standing, and the Siege Onager had launched one shot, where its 60-tick reload allows five. That third defect was not in the report: the reload counted down only in the attack step for an entity target.

**DE, from a reference.** The AoE2 wiki's "Attack ground" page (read 2026-09-24 in the browser pane) lists the mangonel line, the Bombard Cannon, the Trebuchet, the Cannon Galleon and Turtle Ship lines and units this game lacks; not the Demolition Ship line or the Petard. So a Demolition Ship's order is refused. The Trebuchet, Bombard Cannon and the two ship lines are refused too, because their shots have no blast here, and §10.7 records that divergence.

**Review, and what it changed.** An independent read-only review of `e4950559` (one Claude subagent, Opus 5.5 at max, 42 minutes; the Codex lane was at its usage limit until 2026-09-26) found no blocker and six should-fixes, all taken. (1) The census asked only the blast units, so `canAttackGround = firesProjectile` passed every test: a sweep now asks the validator about every unit with no blast, and names 41 accepted under that mutation. (2) The census assumed where a shot lands, so a DE-faithful landing rule would have read as a broken blast: it now reads the landing point from the shot, requires every unit within the radius of it hurt and every other spared, and names the fixture's witnesses when the landing point moves; §10.7 records that a shot at a building lands on its anchor cell and a detonation on its target, where DE centres them on the building and on the ship. (3) The spec's rule was false for attacks on wildlife, which land at once with no shot and no blast: the rule now names units, buildings and the ground, and wildlife is in the bounds. (4) The witness placement threw a bare TypeError for a radius under a cell (the Bombard Cannon's and the Petard's 0.5 are both in units.csv): it now fails by name. (5) The version clashed with v0.3.235, landed first: this is v0.3.236. (6) The Siege Onager's missing minimum range became harmful once its shot blasted, since a point-blank stone would land on its own escorts: it now has units.csv's 3. Nits taken: a stale API comment, "A archer" in the refusal with no unit id, "defect register 2026-09-24" citations that match eight entries, the browser spec importing the whole scenario registry (the layout module now serves it), "hurts everything" in the changelog where the blast hurts units only, and a mutation count that disagreed between the commit and this entry.

**Focused re-review, and what it changed.** The same reviewer read the fixes at `6e39ad3c` (32 minutes, no tests run, the gate being on the machine). No blocker; one should-fix, taken. Judging the blast from the shot's own landing point had cut the tie to the cell aimed at: a ground shot that drifted a cell west passed all three mangonel-line ground cases, the inside witness then standing on the landing cell and the outside one within reach of it. A shot at a unit or at the ground must now land on the cell it was aimed at, where this game and DE agree, and one at a building on the building's footprint, which this game's anchor and DE's centre both satisfy. Nits taken: the census now checks that no unit leaves its cell before the attack lands (it judges each at the cell it began on, and a unit moving on the landing tick moves before the shot resolves); the landing tick is the engine's tick less one, since it advances after the systems run; the sweep lists each non-refusal with its code, so a stub world that falls behind the validator shows as that; the scenario registry imports the seed from the layout module, not through the fixture's re-export; the archived devlog now points here; and the hit-census comment no longer claims a check only the blast census makes.

**Gates and mutations.** After the review, eleven unit-level mutations, each applied by a scratch script, run and restored byte for byte, all red: the old hand list (4: the Siege Onager's three census cases and the unit test); the old validator (both demolition-ship ground cases); every shooter allowed (the sweep, 41 units named); no blast when an area shot lands (all nine mangonel-line cases); the radius stretched by 0.6 (13 cases, each naming a unit hurt outside the radius); each detonation aimed away (two cases each); a shot at a building aimed one cell off its anchor (the three mangonel-line building cases, each naming where it landed); the reload countdown removed ("shots launched at ticks 0"); the step's guard removed (the old-save case); the Siege Onager's minimum range removed (the live hold-fire case and the table check). Before the review, the browser spec, each arm a rebuilt `dist/` on its own preview port: all three old defects, and the countdown alone, both red at "shots launched at ticks 0"; the old hand list and validator with the countdown kept, red at "the stones must kill the Villager inside the blast and spare the one outside it". The hit-census scenario, on a throwaway worktree holding v0.3.235's code and this change's: red with the old hand list ("no one blow reached 2 targets"). After the re-review, seven more against the final census, all red and restored: a ground shot a cell west, a shot at a unit a cell off and a shot at a building a cell west of it (three cases each, each naming where it landed); a shot at a building a cell off its anchor but still on it (the three building cases, each naming the witness it no longer reached); and, again, the old hand list, no blast on landing and the stretched radius.

## Every building gives its owner sight, from the middle of its footprint (v0.3.237)

**Timestamp:** 2026-09-24 to 2026-09-25 (worktree `building-vision` from `8629c785`, rebased onto `bc0a0bf1`; A/B control worktree `bv-control` at `8629c785`)

**Action.** The owner reported that in `raid-warning-fixture` owner 2's Militia hit the human's House at (44,26) and the human never saw them. Reproduced first in a real match on the dev server (Standard map, driven through the page's test API): a House built at (17,14) lit none of its own four cells once its builder walked home, and two Militia took a Barracks foundation from 1,200 to 711 hit points with nothing of owner 2's drawn. The sight table was a partial map of seven types, and a missing entry meant no sight. Now `structures.csv`'s `line_of_sight` is DE's (read in memory from aoe2techtree at `3bb43b14`; 50 values on 18 structures, every other byte identical; the Outpost's +2 per age confirmed by the SiegeEngineers Aoe2Sheet knowledge base). `BUILDING_VISION_RADIUS` is a total record equal to it. `completedBuildingVision.ts` derives a finished building's radius for the seed path, construction completion and `restoreCompletedBuildingVision` on every load. The real maps no longer hard-code a radius. `syncVisibilitySources` moved to `visibilitySourceSync.ts` and places a building's sight on its footprint's central cell, or four central cells for an even size (`buildingVisionSources.ts`). Spec §12.2, the register (with a rollover), decisions, engine feedback, the tick-cost rule in local-rules, changelog.

**The second defect, found by reading the real opening's visible cells.** Every building saw from its top-left cell: the starting Town Center at (8,8) saw 7 cells past its west and north edges and 4 past its east and south, and a House of sight 2 would still have missed raiders on two of the eight cells beside it. One circle on the cell south-east of an even footprint's centre is still off by 5 cells for a House and 23 for a Town Center. Four circles with the radius `sqrt(max(p^2+q^2) + 0.5)`, over the offsets whose cell centre lies within sight, are exact for every square footprint to a sight of 22 (checked for parities and sights 0-60 in a scratch script; odd-by-even footprints are not exact, and none exist).

**What moved.** Four unit tests went red on the first full run. Three were fixtures or a test tuned to the old geometry: two Monk fixtures gave the Town Center an explicit radius 3 that, measured from its centre, reaches the enemy the test needs hidden (now 1, with a premise check added to the one that had none), and `visibilityMutationBatching` asserted that finishing a House adds no source. The fourth was the content-coverage lab: owner 2 never held a Capped Ram. Both arms were sampled every 250 ticks, identical to tick 18,500. At 18,657 owner 2's idle Monk at (40,24), due east of owner 1's Castle at (29,22) and inside its range, died on a cell the old sight did not reach, with the Castle the only shooter within ten cells (that the Castle killed it is inferred; the probe records what stood near, not what hit). The matches part there. In the new one owner 2 loses 62 villagers between 30,000 and 39,000 against 3, 54 of them with owner 1's Arbalests within four cells, attacking in 662 of the 665 times the probe logged one. The census was re-recorded (owner 1 gains the Cavalier, its upgrade, Blast Furnace and Sappers; owner 2 loses the Capped Ram, the Elite Skirmisher and its upgrade, and the Siege Workshop as a producer; union 14/21/34 to 14/20/34), and the open 2026-09-10 register entry carries the new figures. The default-configuration audit (three seeds, 45,000 ticks) moved both ways: Imperial 3 to 2 seats (one seat 5,500 ticks earlier, one not at all), villagers lost 107 to 89, 0 to 0 under enemy defences.

**Measured cost, and the first measurement withdrawn.** The first A/B profiled each arm's own self-play game and read `syncVisibilitySources` doubling (2.9% to 6.4% of CPU on the boot map, 1.5% to 3.0% with eight players). Those were different games: building sight changes play, and at the profiled tick the candidate had 71 units and 45 buildings against the control's 49 and 39 (Fortress 85/119 against 94/117; eight players 149/127 against 184/121). The share's file filter also missed the control's sync, which lives in `visibility.ts`. Withdrawn. What replaced it loads one saved world in both arms: the control's save at tick 30,000 (20,000 for eight players), 2,000 ticks profiled (1,000 for eight players), interleaved, two rounds. `scripts/profile-selfplay.mjs` gained `--save` and `--load` for it, and local-rules' tick-cost rule now says both arms load.
- The worst case first. A stress probe (148 buildings of owner 1 with Town Watch and Town Patrol, 30 Militia a side) found one recompute of that base at up to 9.2 ms against 0.54, and fight ticks at up to 6.4 ms of recompute against 0.2, with every building in the units' map; self-play never shows it, because the AI researches neither technology. Each owner's buildings now see from an engine map of their own (`layeredVisibilityMap.ts`), which a unit's move never recomputes: the fight's recompute is back at the control's, at most 0.14 ms a tick against 0.13.
- On one world the sight code then cost what the control's does (4.30/4.89% of CPU against 4.32/4.70% on the boot map; Fortress 4.85/4.58% against 4.68/4.49%), but the candidate's ticks were still slower. The breakdown found the engine's own state walks: it fingerprints every world-state slot twice a tick, written or not, and validates each slot written, and the building map rode in `aoe2.visibility`, rewritten whenever a unit moves. That was +0.27 ms/tick in `clearStateDirty` and `getStateDirty` on the boot map, and the 20,000-tick bundle went from 284.6 MB to 351.3 MB.
- A slot of its own, `aoe2.buildingVisibility`, written only when a building's sight changes, stopped the validation but not the fingerprinting. So the slot keeps no sources (the first sync after a load places them again) and only the explored cells none of the owner's units explored. The world's state at the boot map's tick 30,000 is then 52.6 KB against the control's 52.2 KB, the two state walks cost +0.03 ms/tick, and the 20,000-tick bundle is 287.3 MB against 287.9 MB, both recorded with the same command.
- What is left: sight questions now ask two maps, about +0.1 ms/tick in the render projection's cell lists (the sight code 4.59/4.91% against 4.34/4.44%). The stress fixture's step median is still +0.4 ms (1.5 against 1.1), mostly because its 148 buildings, which no unit ever stood beside, now see themselves and so enter the human's fog memory (+22 KB of state); in a real game the builders put them there, and on the boot-map world the fog memory is byte-identical in both arms.
- Found on the way and filed as engine asks: the engine fingerprints all state twice a tick (about 0.65 ms of a 6 ms boot-map tick), and `getState` sorts every player's explored cells on every call (3.8% of CPU in a world that grew live, 0.9% in the same world loaded from a save, whose sets come back sorted).

**Gates and mutations.** Seven test files, 30 cases: `structureLineOfSight` (4), `buildingVisionSources` (4), `buildingVision` (8), `visibilitySourceSync` (5), `layeredVisibilityMap` (5), `buildingVisibilitySlot` (2), `projectileDestroysBuilding` (2). 26 mutations on the final code, each red through the case written for it, the tree hashed equal before and after, 30 of 30 green on the restored tree (`gate-proofs.md`).
