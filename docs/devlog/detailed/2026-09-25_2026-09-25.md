## The Siege Onager splashes, and attack-ground fires on every reload and only for units whose shot blasts (v0.3.236)

**Timestamp:** 2026-09-24 to 25 (worktree `siege-onager-blast`, begun on `1ea83922` and rebased three times: onto `6a7ad265`, the attack-warning lane's v0.3.235, and last onto `04fee601`, the owner decisions and the gate-lock rule)

**Action.** `isAreaProjectile` is every projectile attacker with a blast radius, read from the blast table; its own list, `['mangonel', 'onager']`, is gone. `canAttackGround` (a unit whose shot blasts where it lands, which is the mangonel line) gates the attack-ground validator, whose refusal now names the unit and its id, and the attack-ground step, which drops an order its unit cannot carry out. The step counts the reload down. The Siege Onager holds fire inside 3 tiles (units.csv: 3-8). New: `fixtures/blastCensus.ts` and its layout `fixtures/blastCensusLayout.ts`, one fixture per unit in the blast table, registered from the table; `tests/simulation/blastCensus.test.ts`; three cases in `attackGround.test.ts`; `tests/browser/attack-ground-hotkey.spec.ts`, the first browser spec to give the order through G and a click; and a Siege Onager scenario in `playerHitCensus.test.ts`, which landed on main with v0.3.235. Spec §10.3 and §10.7, the defect register (with the rollover of the 2026-09-23 stats-files entry, main having rolled the prices entry first), the changelog, and this file's rollover.

**Reproduced first, on a headless bridge.** The census, written before the fix, failed 5 of its 15 attack cases: the Siege Onager's attack on a unit landed by tick 10 and spared the witness 1.41 cells out, inside its 1.5 radius; on a House, by tick 14, the same; its ground order hurt nothing in 600 ticks; both demolition ships' ground orders were accepted. A throwaway probe (deleted) read the rest: after 300 ticks under a ground order the Demolition Ship was alive and still ordered, with every enemy standing, and the Siege Onager had launched one shot, where its 60-tick reload allows five. That third defect was not in the report: the reload counted down only in the attack step for an entity target.

**DE, from a reference.** The AoE2 wiki's "Attack ground" page (read 2026-09-24 in the browser pane) lists the mangonel line, the Bombard Cannon, the Trebuchet, the Cannon Galleon and Turtle Ship lines and units this game lacks; not the Demolition Ship line or the Petard. So a Demolition Ship's order is refused. The Trebuchet, Bombard Cannon and the two ship lines are refused too, because their shots have no blast here, and §10.7 records that divergence.

**Review, and what it changed.** An independent read-only review of `e4950559` (one Claude subagent, Opus 5.5 at max, 42 minutes; the Codex lane was at its usage limit until 2026-09-26) found no blocker and six should-fixes, all taken. (1) The census asked only the blast units, so `canAttackGround = firesProjectile` passed every test: a sweep now asks the validator about every unit with no blast, and names 41 accepted under that mutation. (2) The census assumed where a shot lands, so a DE-faithful landing rule would have read as a broken blast: it now reads the landing point from the shot, requires every unit within the radius of it hurt and every other spared, and names the fixture's witnesses when the landing point moves; §10.7 records that a shot at a building lands on its anchor cell and a detonation on its target, where DE centres them on the building and on the ship. (3) The spec's rule was false for attacks on wildlife, which land at once with no shot and no blast: the rule now names units, buildings and the ground, and wildlife is in the bounds. (4) The witness placement threw a bare TypeError for a radius under a cell (the Bombard Cannon's and the Petard's 0.5 are both in units.csv): it now fails by name. (5) The version clashed with v0.3.235, landed first: this is v0.3.236. (6) The Siege Onager's missing minimum range became harmful once its shot blasted, since a point-blank stone would land on its own escorts: it now has units.csv's 3. Nits taken: a stale API comment, "A archer" in the refusal with no unit id, "defect register 2026-09-24" citations that match eight entries, the browser spec importing the whole scenario registry (the layout module now serves it), "hurts everything" in the changelog where the blast hurts units only, and a mutation count that disagreed between the commit and this entry.

**Focused re-review, and what it changed.** The same reviewer read the fixes at `6e39ad3c` (32 minutes, no tests run, the gate being on the machine). No blocker; one should-fix, taken. A shot at a unit or at the ground must now land on the cell it was aimed at, where this game and DE agree, and one at a building on the building's footprint, which this game's anchor and DE's centre both satisfy. Nits taken: the census now checks that no unit leaves its cell before the attack lands (it judges each at the cell it began on, and a unit moving on the landing tick moves before the shot resolves); the landing tick is the engine's tick less one, since it advances after the systems run; the sweep lists each non-refusal with its code, so a stub world that falls behind the validator shows as that; the scenario registry imports the seed from the layout module, not through the fixture's re-export; the archived devlog now points here; and the hit-census comment no longer claims a check only the blast census makes.

## On a CPU rasteriser the Natural ground draws one surface sample per pixel (v0.3.237)

**Timestamp:** 2026-09-25 (the natural-tier lane, milestone 1, worktree `natural-tier` on `1ea83922`, rebased onto `6a7ad265`)

**Why.** v0.3.233 made Natural the default and main's CI went red: the ground's blend doubled the live frame on SwiftShader (233.3 ms against Moebius's 116.6), every browser shard ran 50-60% longer and two frame-timed specs failed; v0.3.234 put Moebius back. The debugging record (`docs/debugging/2026-09-24-de-ground-swiftshader-cost.md`) named four ways out, and the coordinator chose the software tier.

**Action.** `src/rendering/voxel/aoeDeGroundTier.ts` reads the renderer the game's own WebGL context names (`WEBGL_debug_renderer_info`, else `RENDERER`) from the context the runtime's renderer factory builds, and again on `webglcontextrestored`. A CPU rasteriser (SwiftShader, WARP, llvmpipe, softpipe, lavapipe, Apple's software renderer) gets `single-sample`, anything else and an unnamed renderer `blend`; `aoe2:de-ground-tier` in localStorage overrides it when the renderer is built. The tier is a define in the ground's one material. The single-sample shader reads its own cell, one linearly filtered fields texture and the macro field, and one surface texel: dirt where the dirt field passes a half, sand within about a quarter tile of known water, else its own kind. The fog texture became that fields texture (RGBA: fog level, known mask, dirt, known water); the blend reads its R only. `AoeVoxelRendererState` reports `groundTier` and `rasteriser`. `scripts/captureMapScreenshot.mjs` takes `GROUND_TIER`.

**What SwiftShader charges for, measured.** One page, both styles, four masked CPUs, variants built and restored by one job. A constant-colour ground drew the frame at 0.76 of Moebius; the one surface read was nearly all the rest: `textureGrad` (explicit gradients, which the blend needs inside branches) cost 0.18 of Moebius's whole frame more than the same read with the rasteriser's own derivatives, and texelFetch or a bilinear 2D read cost about the same as the implicit read. The macro read, the fields read and the tint cost nothing measurable. So the one read takes an explicit mip level worked out from `dFdx`/`dFdy` at the top of the function, inside a loop a quad of unexplored fragments never enters.

**Instrument, and the one discarded.** The debugging record's method (animation-frame start to a one-pixel read in a later callback) counts the compositor's work on the frame before, the same in both styles. With the HUD's frosted glass on, it read the single-sample tier at 0.80 to 1.01 of Moebius on four masked CPUs (one red run with nothing wrong) and 0.92 to 0.98 on CI (run 36096561813). The gate instead wraps every animation-frame callback, drains the GPU process's queue with a one-pixel read before it, reads again after it, and counts only callbacks that drew a world frame; the blur is switched off for the run. Style switches call the menu row's own handler: Playwright's actionability waits cost seconds per switch on SwiftShader, and `art-style-setting.spec.ts` drives the real click.

**Frame cost per tier (the paused 800x600 opening view, lower quartile, Natural over Moebius).** CI, run 36100866192 (four vCPUs, SwiftShader Subzero): single-sample 0.85-0.87 (four readings), blend 2.12, about 12 s a reading. Development machine held to four CPUs: 0.81-0.83 and 1.60-2.04. Its 32 threads: 0.59-0.61 and 0.97-1.40. RTX 4090 at 1920x1080 (the debugging record's figures, not re-measured): 2.2-2.7 ms in every arm. The local figures were taken on a shared machine whose other sessions' load was not recorded; one ran a SwiftShader sweep on about 13 cores during part of the window.

**Pixels.** Captures on both rasterisers, before (`1ea83922`) and after, five Natural views and two Moebius views. GPU Natural: 0 pixels changed in all five (800x600, zoom 0.7 at 1280x720, zoom 2.4, the terrain showcase, 1920x1080). Moebius: 0 on both rasterisers. The blend forced on SwiftShader: 0 in all five against v0.3.232's. SwiftShader Natural, the new tier: 20.17%, 3.34%, 25.82%, 13.32% and 4.33%, all of it ground: hard tile edges between kinds, thin hard-edged sand along the water, dirt around the Town Center, the fog rim still soft on the known side. The terrain showcase is behind its victory dialog at tick 1, so it says little. Looked at by eye at native resolution: the default view, zoom 2.4, a crop of the right-hand shore and rim, and the 1920x1080 explored area.

**Gates and mutations.** `tests/browser/de-ground-frame-cost.spec.ts` retires the SwiftShader lesson: red at 2.040 on four CPUs and 1.063 on 32 threads with SwiftShader taken out of the CPU pattern, and at 1.875 and 1.006 with the single-sample branch compiled out while the page still reports that tier. `tests/browser/de-ground-full-blend.spec.ts` forces the blend on SwiftShader and is red under four mutations: the override ignored, the blend's colour scaled, its fog fade made a cut, its GLSL broken. Unit files: `aoeDeGroundTier.test.ts`, `AoeVoxelWorldRendererGroundTier.test.ts`, and the data and mesh tests for the fields texture and the per-tier programs. Two masked mutation runs went red at boot instead (the five-second snapshot poll in `waitForPausedBootWithSeed` outlasted by blend-slow frames); the gate now boots with one light read, and the runs were repeated.

**The independent review.** One lane: a read-only Claude subagent (Opus 5.5, the session's own model and effort) on the exact commit; Codex is at its account limit until 2026-09-26. No blocker. Taken: a duplicated drift-log row from the rebase; the capture script accepting `GROUND_TIER` over a Moebius frame (it now refuses unless the textured ground draws, and the cited commands say `STYLE=de`); the single-sample fade reaching half brightness against a black cell at an inner corner of the rim (it is now the square of its smoothstep, a quarter there, and the spec says so); the single-sample tier's rim checked in pixels on every machine (`de-ground-full-blend.spec.ts` samples it on its second page; red with the fade removed); the frame-cost gate asserting that the ground is textured and lit (red, with the verdict at 0.458, when the mesh never shows); a light boot for both specs (the first full gate after these fixes went red on the new ground check, which read the renderer right after the switch, before a frame had presented it, and saw Moebius's twelve chunks; it now reads after the timed frames, and a mutation drawing Natural on the voxel ground turns it red); honest wording about what the first `readPixels` drains; the tier header quoting the gate's figures; the packing cost re-measured (no change); and two caveats in the gate proof (the check is relative, and the flip is the next commit).
