## On a CPU rasteriser the Natural ground draws one surface sample per pixel (v0.3.237)

**Timestamp:** 2026-09-25 (the natural-tier lane, milestone 1, worktree `natural-tier` on `1ea83922`, rebased onto `6a7ad265`)

**Why.** v0.3.233 made Natural the default and main's CI went red: the ground's blend doubled the live frame on SwiftShader (233.3 ms against Moebius's 116.6), every browser shard ran 50-60% longer and two frame-timed specs failed; v0.3.234 put Moebius back. The debugging record (`docs/debugging/2026-09-24-de-ground-swiftshader-cost.md`) named four ways out, and the coordinator chose the software tier.

**Action.** `src/rendering/voxel/aoeDeGroundTier.ts` reads the renderer the game's own WebGL context names (`WEBGL_debug_renderer_info`, else `RENDERER`) from the context the runtime's renderer factory builds, and again on `webglcontextrestored`. A CPU rasteriser (SwiftShader, WARP, llvmpipe, softpipe, lavapipe, Apple's software renderer) gets `single-sample`, anything else and an unnamed renderer `blend`; `aoe2:de-ground-tier` in localStorage overrides it when the renderer is built. The tier is a define in the ground's one material. The single-sample shader reads its own cell, one linearly filtered fields texture and the macro field, and one surface texel: dirt where the dirt field passes a half, sand within about a quarter tile of known water, else its own kind. The fog texture became that fields texture (RGBA: fog level, known mask, dirt, known water); the blend reads its R only. `AoeVoxelRendererState` reports `groundTier` and `rasteriser`. `scripts/captureMapScreenshot.mjs` takes `GROUND_TIER`.

**What SwiftShader charges for, measured.** One page, both styles, four masked CPUs, variants built and restored by one job. A constant-colour ground drew the frame at 0.76 of Moebius; the one surface read was nearly all the rest: `textureGrad` (explicit gradients, which the blend needs inside branches) cost 0.18 of Moebius's whole frame more than the same read with the rasteriser's own derivatives, and texelFetch or a bilinear 2D read cost about the same as the implicit read. The macro read, the fields read and the tint cost nothing measurable. So the one read takes an explicit mip level worked out from `dFdx`/`dFdy` at the top of the function, inside a loop a quad of unexplored fragments never enters.

**Instrument, and the one discarded.** The debugging record's method (animation-frame start to a one-pixel read in a later callback) counts the compositor's work on the frame before, the same in both styles. With the HUD's frosted glass on, it read the single-sample tier at 0.80 to 1.01 of Moebius on four masked CPUs (one red run with nothing wrong) and 0.92 to 0.98 on CI (run 36096561813). The gate instead wraps every animation-frame callback, drains the GPU process's queue with a one-pixel read before it, reads again after it, and counts only callbacks that drew a world frame; the blur is switched off for the run. Style switches call the menu row's own handler: Playwright's actionability waits cost seconds per switch on SwiftShader, and `art-style-setting.spec.ts` drives the real click.

**Frame cost per tier (the paused 800x600 opening view, lower quartile, Natural over Moebius).** CI, run 36100866192 (four vCPUs, SwiftShader Subzero): single-sample 0.85-0.87 (four readings), blend 2.12, about 12 s a reading. Development machine held to four CPUs: 0.81-0.83 and 1.60-2.04. Its 32 threads: 0.59-0.61 and 0.97-1.40. RTX 4090 at 1920x1080 (the debugging record's figures, not re-measured): 2.2-2.7 ms in every arm. The local figures were taken on a shared machine whose other sessions' load was not recorded; one ran a SwiftShader sweep on about 13 cores during part of the window.

**Pixels.** Captures on both rasterisers, before (`1ea83922`) and after, five Natural views and two Moebius views. GPU Natural: 0 pixels changed in all five (800x600, zoom 0.7 at 1280x720, zoom 2.4, the terrain showcase, 1920x1080). Moebius: 0 on both rasterisers. The blend forced on SwiftShader: 0 in all five against v0.3.232's. SwiftShader Natural, the new tier: 20.17%, 3.34%, 25.82%, 13.32% and 4.33%, all of it ground: hard tile edges between kinds, thin hard-edged sand along the water, dirt around the Town Center, the fog rim still soft on the known side. The terrain showcase is behind its victory dialog at tick 1, so it says little. Looked at by eye at native resolution: the default view, zoom 2.4, a crop of the right-hand shore and rim, and the 1920x1080 explored area.

**Gates and mutations.** `tests/browser/de-ground-frame-cost.spec.ts` retires the SwiftShader lesson: red at 2.040 on four CPUs and 1.063 on 32 threads with SwiftShader taken out of the CPU pattern, and at 1.875 and 1.006 with the single-sample branch compiled out while the page still reports that tier. `tests/browser/de-ground-full-blend.spec.ts` forces the blend on SwiftShader and is red under four mutations: the override ignored, the blend's colour scaled, its fog fade made a cut, its GLSL broken. **The independent review.** One lane: a read-only Claude subagent (Opus 5.5, the session's own model and effort) on the exact commit; Codex is at its account limit until 2026-09-26. No blocker.
