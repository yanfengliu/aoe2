## 2026-10-12 — Work 122: placing a building on unexplored ground follows Definitive Edition

**Timestamp and owner.** 2026-10-11 01:34 to 2026-10-12, local (America/Los_Angeles), cut twice by the account's session limit. Implementation worker for work 122 ([plan](../../work/122_unexplored-placement/plan.md)), worktree `unexplored-placement`, version vNEXT.

**Sources.** Work 118's design named three official notes, and each was read on ageofempires.com: update 44725 (2021-01-25: a blueprint over unexplored terrain no longer reveals whether a building or unit occupies it; it can be placed and is not buildable where obstructed), hotfix 45185 (2021-02-11: placement allowed when part of the footprint is explored, refused when all of it is unexplored; no start while overlapping an unseen structure or foundation) and update 46295 (2021-03-24: a foundation over an obstacle the fog hid is cancelled when villagers try to begin construction). Whether DE refunds a cancelled unstarted foundation is not in the notes; forum reports say deleting one returns its cost, and this work refunds it.

**Reproduction.** Instrument: the bridge's own placement queries, the calls the HUD and the view make (`beginBuildingPlacement`, `getPlacementPreview`), in `tmp/probes/122-ghostOverUnexplored.mts`. On `aoe2-prototype` at tick 0 a house on wholly unexplored ground was red on 86 of 86 water footprints, 41 of 41 forest and 10 of 10 enemy-building footprints, and green on 228 of 228 open grass and 20 of 20 hill.

**Action.** `bridge/placementKnowledge.ts` wraps the three placement answers (`isPlacementBlocked`, `describePlacementBlockers`, `findOpenPlacementAnchors`) and chooses by the builders' owner: the human seat, unless an AI drives it, reads only explored ground (its own and its allies', the cells the frame does not draw black); every AI seat reads the true map as before. The footprint rule moved from `cellPassability.ts` to `bridge/placementFootprint.ts` with a view: an unexplored cell under the footprint blocks nothing, one beside it meets no requirement (a Dock needs seen water), and off-map cells stay known. A footprint with no explored cell is refused (`placement_unexplored`, with its own message); a refusal counts and names only explored cells, and a Dock or Fish Trap refused by its own ground rule now says which rule instead of blaming "an obstacle"; reachability floods explored ground only. `bridge/foundationStart.ts` checks a foundation's ground on the true map, its own claims left out, when the first builder arrives at progress 0 (not a Farm's: it is walkable and one cell); if anything stands there it removes the foundation through the Delete key's door, refunds the owner's current price and says what was found and where. It is wired from `wirePreSeedOps` to `builderWorkStep`. A new fixture pair (`unexplored-placement-open-fixture`, `unexplored-placement-hidden-fixture`) differs only under the human's fog. `scripts/captureMapScreenshot.mjs` gained `PLACE="house@x,y"` (the ghost a player sees at a cell), and `diffMapScreenshots.mjs` prints where the changed pixels lie.
