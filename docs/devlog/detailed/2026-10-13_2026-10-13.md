**Reasoning.** The DE notes rule out an enemy seeing an unstarted foundation in any way: 61321 outside a treaty, and 199626's expected result. So hiding covers every channel, Monk conversion included, and fog memory forgets an ally's too, as 46295 says. They say nothing about an ally in plain sight, so that stays. Spies follows the viewer's side rather than whose eyes see the ground, which is the reading of "in any way"; no note covers Spies. Conquest changed with it. Once no enemy can find an unstarted foundation, one that kept its owner in the match would leave a decided match unable to end, and update 46295 stopped a Donjon foundation doing exactly that. Reading the note as "a started foundation does keep its owner in" is unverified, so that stays as it was. Each refusal sits at a choke point (`setUnitAttackCommandDirect` and the attack step, the Monk's order and its lane), and the finders skip the foundation too, so a soldier moves on to the next target instead of being refused forever.
