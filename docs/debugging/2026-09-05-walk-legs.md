# Debugging session — soldiers do not move their legs while walking

## Symptom
Owner report, 2026-09-05: "soldiers are not moving their legs during the walking animation." Military humanoids (infantry, archers) walk across the map with their legs apparently frozen. Reproduced on `?seed=unit-showcase-fixture` (vite preview of commit c0b1e350, port 4181): villager (5,12)->(4,24), militia (7,16)->(6,24), champion (8,12)->(8,24), archer (11,16)->(10,24), knight (14,12)->(14,24), all ordered south at tick 0 and sampled at ticks 40-45.

## Expected vs actual
- Expected: a walking soldier's boots reach and lift in opposition and its legs counter-swing, as authored in `poseHumanoidPart` (boot reach ±0.15 × scale, lift 0.12 × scale, leg pitch 0.5 rad at full locomotion weight) — the walk the 2026-07-11 devlog describes as "boots visibly reach and lift in opposition".
- Actual: every walker reaches `mode: 'moving'` with the gait phase advancing, but `locomotionWeight` settles at 0.19-0.30 (villager), 0.24-0.33 (militia), 0.31-0.39 (champion), 0.26-0.30 (archer), 0.40-0.51 (knight). The pose multiplies every amplitude by that weight, so a militia's boot travels about 0.03 tiles — about one pixel at the default zoom. The villager is affected identically; the owner noticed it on soldiers because soldiers are what a player watches walking for whole screens at a time.

## Reproduction
- Engine debug tool, named before any probe of my own: **none of the replay tools answer this.** `replay:inspect`, `diffBundles` and `SessionReplayer.forkAt` read simulation ground truth (positions, plans, occupancy); the leg pose is a renderer-side function of the DISPLAYED root and the per-identity motion history, which no replay re-simulates. The instrument that does answer it is the shipped browser test API: `window.__AOE2_TEST__.inspectVoxelUnitMotion(identity)` reads the live `AoeUnitMotionHistory` (mode, locomotionWeight, gaitPhaseRadians, speed) for a unit, and `advanceTicks` steps the presentation exactly. `inspectPresentedVoxelPartMatrix` would read the posed part matrix, but it needs a presented frame and the hidden Browser pane never gets an animation frame, so it returned null here. After that: a unit test over `resolveUnitAnimationState` + `createUnitParts` (the gate) and the maintained capture pair for pixels.
- Commands: `npm run build`; `npm run preview -- --host 127.0.0.1 --port 4181 --strictPort`; open `http://127.0.0.1:4181/?seed=unit-showcase-fixture`; in the page: `setPaused(true)`, five `selectEntityAtCell` + `issueMoveCommand` orders above, `advanceTicks(40)`, then per half-tick `advanceTicks(1, 50)` reading `getDisplayedEntities()` and `inspectVoxelUnitMotion('<id>:0')`.
- No temporary instrumentation in the tree; the readout script lived only in the Browser pane's JS console. `tmp/cropPair.mjs` (ignored) crops a before/after pair side by side at 3x for the leg inspection below.

## Hypotheses
- [x] (1) `locomotionWeight` is low for every walker: CONFIRMED by the readout above. `resolveUnitAnimationState` blended the weight toward `clamp01(speed / FULL_LOCOMOTION_SPEED)` with `FULL_LOCOMOTION_SPEED = 2.5` tiles/s. That constant is the pre-§12.4.2 uniform unit speed; v0.3.160 (2026-08-28) moved the clock to `round(0.32 × speedPercent)` hundredths of a fine step per tick — villager 0.8 tiles/s, militia 0.9, archer 0.96, knight 1.35 — so no unit in the game reaches the speed the weight was normalised against. Weight at base rate: 0.32 villager, 0.36 militia, 0.54 knight, exactly the numbers read out of the live game.
- [x] (2) attack pose or reach correction overwrites the gait: RULED OUT — `poseUnitAttackParts` returns the parts untouched at `attackWeight` 0, and no sampled unit had an attack event (`mode` was `moving`, never `attacking`).
- [x] (3) a cached recipe bypasses `posePart`: RULED OUT — `createUnitParts` runs `animateUnitParts` on every presented frame with the resolved state (`aoeVoxelAdapter.prepareInstanceEntities`), and the gait phase advanced every frame in the readout.
- [x] (4) legs move but armour covers them: RULED OUT by geometry — the mail skirt sits at y 0.42-0.70 and the tunic above 0.5, while the legs span y 0.13-0.55 and the boots 0-0.13, so the lower legs and boots are exposed on every infantry recipe; and the villager, with nothing covering its legs, reads the same weight.
- [x] (5) soldier-only path: RULED OUT — `poseHumanoidPart` is shared by villager, infantry and archer and there is no role branch in the state machine; the defect is every walker's and shows most on the units that walk furthest.

## Investigation log
- 2026-09-05 — Read `aoeVoxelUnitLocomotionPose.ts`, `aoeVoxelUnitAnimation.ts`, the humanoid/mounted recipes, the attack rigs and pose styles, the adapter's per-frame path and the smoother. Nothing distinguishes a soldier from a villager anywhere on the pose path.
- 2026-09-05 — Real game first: built the tree at c0b1e350, previewed on 4181, staged the five-unit southward walk on `unit-showcase-fixture`. All five `moving`, gait phase advancing ~0.09-0.12 rad per half tick, weights 0.19-0.51, window speeds 0.5-1.3 tiles/s. Screenshots at zoom 2.0 and 2.4 in the Browser pane: units translate across the grass with legs in the rest pose.
- 2026-09-05 — Devlog history: v0.3.160 "movement clock to §12.4.2 (0.32 fine/tick = villager 0.8 tiles/s ...)" and v0.3.192 "a 3.3 Hz pulse the v0.3.160 gait window had hidden from the LEGS but not the BODY". Before v0.3.160 units moved at 2.5 tiles/s and the weight reached 1; the pose amplitudes were tuned then and never re-normalised.
- 2026-09-05 — Gate written first (`tests/rendering/aoeVoxelUnitWalkLegs.test.ts`) and run on the unfixed tree: 158 failed, 1 passed (the instrument check that a villager is driven at 0.8 tiles/s). Every failure names the pre-fix weight: `villager ... reached locomotion weight 0.320`, `elite-teutonic-knight -boot-left travels 0.084 x scale ... at locomotion weight 0.260`, `elite-war-elephant horse-leg-front-left travels 0.094 x scale ... at 0.240`. Saved as `tmp/walk-legs-red.txt`.
- 2026-09-05 — After the fix, the same five units read weight 1.000 on all 24 samples each (window speeds 0.71-1.07 tiles/s); a fresh straight walk for the knight, cavalry-archer and scout read 0.972-1.0, 0.964-1.0 and 0.955-1.0 over 30 samples with none below 0.95, so the 2-tick cavalry cadence's window ripple (1.2-1.75 tiles/s measured against a 1.35 base) does not flutter the gait at the three-quarter normaliser.

## Root cause
`FULL_LOCOMOTION_SPEED = 2.5` tiles/s in `resolveUnitAnimationState` is the retired uniform clock; at the §12.4.2 rates every unit walks at 0.5-1.6 tiles/s, so the locomotion weight — which scales every gait amplitude — never exceeds 0.2-0.6 and the legs swing at a fraction of the authored motion, below one pixel at the default zoom for a humanoid.

## Fix
- `src/rendering/unitStepCadence.ts`: `unitBaseTilesPerSecond(entityType)` beside the cadence helper — the hundredths a unit banks per tick at its base rate, as quarter-tile fine steps, at TPS ticks a second (0.8 villager, 0.9 militia, 1.35 knight; wildlife reads as a villager, as the cadence already does).
- `src/rendering/voxel/aoeVoxelUnitAnimation.ts`: the flat constant is gone; the target weight is `clamp01(speed / (0.75 × unitBaseTilesPerSecond(entity.entityType)))`. Full gait at three quarters of the unit's OWN base rate, because the drawn root's window speed ripples with the carry's 3-tick/4-tick alternation (0.62-0.83 tiles/s for a villager at 0.8) and a weight that reads 1 at the trough does not flutter with it; below that the unit is being slowed (traffic, a door ball) and the gait fades, which is the fade the weight exists for. Movement technologies only make a unit faster and clamp at 1.
- The pose amplitudes in `aoeVoxelUnitLocomotionPose.ts` are untouched: they were authored for weight 1 and the fix restores weight 1. This is the root cause, not the symptom: raising the amplitudes would have hidden the same stale normaliser behind a bigger swing and left every slowed-unit fade three times too eager.
- `tests/rendering/aoeVoxelUnitAnimation.test.ts` (`is split-distance invariant and does not advance phase while stopped`): its "stopped weight has started to decay at 300 ms" assertion held only because the old normaliser mapped a 2.1 tiles/s burst to 0.84; the two-anchor window still reads that burst as walking at 300 ms, so the assertion now reads the first samples after the window has emptied (through the 450 ms anchor promotions), which is what "stopped" means to this state machine. That file now sits at exactly 500 lines, the hard cap; the next addition to it has to split it.
