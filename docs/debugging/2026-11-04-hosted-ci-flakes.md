# Debugging session — three hosted CI flakes (resource workers, boar strikes, art-style headroom)

## Symptom

Main's hosted CI (ubuntu-latest browser shards, SwiftShader) failed at random on commits that changed only `.md` files:

- `tests/browser/resource-workers.spec.ts:100` at 1920px, 3 of 101 runs since 2026-10-01, all after work 123 sped the test up on 2026-10-14 (3 of the 51 runs since, 0 of the 50 before): runs 38675522945 and 39193369383 expected "Moving" and read "Gathering food" 4 times, then "Idle" 5 times; run 40960398985 read "Moving", then food workers 0 on all 9 retries.
- `tests/browser/voxel-unit-attack-animation.spec.ts:36`, 1 of 101 runs: run 40983419097 failed `expect(frozenPresentationAfter.parts).toEqual(frozenPresentationBefore.parts)`, where "before" held `null` parts and "after" held revision-13 parts.
- `tests/browser/art-style-setting.spec.ts:174` used 69% (20.8 s) and 67% (20.1 s) of its 30 s limit on shard 1/4 in runs 39383478029 and 39810774781; `scripts/test-time-headroom.mjs` fails a test past two thirds. Across 51 runs its time was 8.6 to 20.8 s, median 14.2 s.

## Expected vs actual

- Expected: each test's verdict is the same on every host.
- Actual: each verdict, or its time, depended on how fast the runner drew frames.

## Reproduction

- Instruments, named first. The recorded-world tools (`bundleHotspots`, `snapshotAtTick`, `replay:inspect`, `diffBundles`) answer nothing here: no failing run recorded a bundle, and both flakes live in live-page timing (HUD writes, Playwright's poll schedule, the renderer's hit-state handover), not in simulation state. The first instrument was each failure's own Playwright trace, read with a snapshot resolver (`evidence/snapshotHud.cjs`) that rebuilds the DOM at every action. Every script, probe and output named here is kept, ignored, under `tmp/ci-flakes-1104-evidence-20261104/` in the primary checkout, with a manifest of bytes and SHA-256; the failure traces stay in `tmp/ci-flake-artifacts-20261104/`. `bridge.getDebugSnapshot()` was the named live instrument for positions; the probes read the same positions from `getRenderState()` per frame, because the question was a time series. Every probe carried a `// harness:` line.
- Hosted rates came from all 404 browser-shard job logs of main's CI runs since 2026-10-01 (101 runs), fetched once with `gh api .../jobs/<id>/logs`.
- Local runs: `PREVIEW_PORT=4311 BROWSER_RASTERISER=swiftshader npx playwright test <spec> --repeat-each N` on a built worktree, plus scratch probes (`probes/`, with their own config). Frame models for a slow host: CDP `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate` 4x and 8x; a 300 ms frame-starvation shim; one frame scheduler whose every frame starts with a long task (a fixed 250 ms, or 100-600 ms at random).

## Hypotheses

- [x] Resource workers: the House anchor sits next to where the worker stands, so the walk the test reads is about one cell and ends inside the checks on a slow host. Confirmed (below).
- [x] Resource workers, work 137's other candidate: a raised entity intercepts the anchor click. Ruled out: the trace's "Moving" proves the move landed, and work 137's click-stack read at (8,6) was empty.
- [x] Boar strikes: the spec's wait (`presentedRevision === acceptedRevision`) passes before the renderer's hit state, which `inspectPresentedVoxelPartMatrix` reads, has caught up. Confirmed (below).
- [ ] Boar strikes, alternative: a WebGL context loss put the runtime in `restoring`. Not supported: the trace has no console line, and the revision arithmetic below accounts for the failure exactly.
- [x] Art-style case 174: some of the test's own steps (its capture, its waits, its order) can be made cheaper without changing a check. Refuted by measurement (below): the cost is GPU work the test cannot avoid.

- 2026-11-04 — Art-style, a CI model. Pinning the Playwright worker and every browser process under it to 4 logical CPUs (`pin-tree.ps1`, which touches only that tree) gives case 174/138 a ratio of 3.2, against 2.4-3.5 on CI. Pinned, case 174 took 4.9-5.5 s first in a worker and 5.7-7.6 s after another case, and its capture 3.1-3.5 s of 4.2-4.6 s of steps. Process CPU sampling: while a paused Moebius page is open, the GPU process keeps about 2 of the 4 CPUs busy, because the live loop redraws the unchanged frame; after the page closes it still works for up to about 2 s. A 3 s idle gap after case 146 brought case 174 back to its first-in-worker time, so that tail is what a following case inherits.
- 2026-11-04 — Art-style, rejected candidates, each measured on the pinned model. (1) Read the live canvas in a frame callback instead of capturing: pixels byte-identical to the capture in both styles, but no faster (first read 3.1-3.5 s either way; warm 2.0-2.5 s against 1.7-1.8 s). Read outside a frame callback it returned an all-zero canvas, which would have passed both styles' "one colour" checks vacuously. (2) Hold the live loop's frames during the read: four interleaved pairs, 4.96-5.29 s against 4.98-5.35 s. (3) Run case 138 between 146 and 174: four interleaved pairs, mean 6.94 to 6.66 s, inside both arms' spread. Splitting, a smaller viewport, another zoom or another order of switch and zoom either repeats the expensive part or changes the bound's cells and camera.

## Root cause

- Resource workers: the test asserted states that end when a one-cell walk ends ("Moving", the walking food occupation, and the House order's effect), and on CI's slow runners the walk ended inside Playwright's polls.
- Boar strikes: the spec waited on the voxel runtime's watermark, which a forced sync with no visual change moves at once, while the presented parts it then read come from the renderer's hit state, which follows only at the next frame.
- Art-style case 174: its time is the first Moebius draw at zoom 0.7 on SwiftShader and the GPU work the live loop keeps queuing for a paused, unchanged frame, plus the previous case's tail. No check-preserving change to the test reduces it measurably.

## Fix

- `tests/browser/resource-workers.spec.ts`: the match must have started at Normal speed, the speed the walk bound is worked out at. The move is ordered once the worker gathers at the sheep, on every host. The House anchor is at least `MIN_WALK_CELLS` = 8 cells from the worker, through a new keep-away option of `findValidPlacementNearTownCenter` that refuses a non-finite point. A setup guard re-reads the worker and asserts that distance before the click. "Moving" and food 1 are read together, and again just before the House order. After the order, the spec requires "Building House" with food 0, which only a build order produces. A walk that ends reads "Idle". Every original assertion stays.
- `tests/browser/helpers/gameTestHelpers/presentation.ts`: `waitForPresentedHitState` waits for a world frame drawn after it began, with the runtime running and the accepted epoch and revision presented. That is `isInteractionReady()` read from outside. `voxel-unit-attack-animation.spec.ts` uses it at all five waits, and now requires the frozen pose's parts to be non-null before comparing them, since null before and after would also be equal.
- `tests/browser/voxel-presented-hit-state.spec.ts`: the gate for the wait. It holds frames with a gate on `requestAnimationFrame` that keeps ids cancellable across the release. It forces two syncs and checks, a round trip later, that the window is open. It then starts the wait and lets frames through only after the wait has read the renderer's state four times without returning, a handshake rather than a timer. Last it requires the part after the wait.

## Verification

- Resource workers: the old anchor under the forced geometry turns the setup guard red at all three widths (1.27 cells, expected at least 8). A one-cell walk failed "still walking as the House is placed" 6 of 6 times with 100-600 ms long tasks per frame, where the 8-cell walk passed 9 of 9. With a two-cell walk and the House click removed, the new "Building House" check failed 3 of 3 widths ("Idle"), while the old food-0 check alone passed 3 of 3: the old check was vacuous whenever the walk ended inside its poll. The spec passed on SwiftShader and on the GPU, and 6 of 6 order cases under the 100-600 ms model.
- Boar strikes: three interleaved rounds of 20 on SwiftShader, unchanged spec 9 of 60 failed (CI's diff each time), fixed spec 0 of 60; after review round 0 changed the wait and added the non-null check, the fixed spec failed 0 of 60 again. The handshake gate failed 10 of 10 with the wait's frame requirement removed, and 10 of 10 with the spec's original wait put back verbatim, each at "the wait returned while frames were held"; it passed 5 of 5 as fixed.
- Review, the full gate and the hosted run are recorded in work 137's and work 139's plans.

## Follow-ups

- No engine gap. The hit-state handover lag lives in this repo's renderer; the window it opens is harmless for real input, so nothing in the product changes.
- `voxel-wildlife-feedback.spec.ts` keeps its own watermark-only wait, but reads presented parts only in a live loop with no forced sync before the read, so it does not open the window today.
- Art-style case 174 stays OPEN (defect register, 2026-11-04). The levers left are product-side: the live loop redraws an unchanged paused frame, and `getWorldCapture` draws twice per capture (`renderer.frame`, then `runtime.capture`'s `renderCurrent`). Or the owner raises its limit with the reason written where it is set.
- The selection panel rebuilds its whole markup whenever the selection state changes, and that state carries the selected unit's position. Playwright's Build House click while the worker walked retried twice on CI ("element was detached from the DOM", 2.2 s). Whether a real player's click can land across such a rebuild was not checked.
