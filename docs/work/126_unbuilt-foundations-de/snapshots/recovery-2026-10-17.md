# Recovery: unstarted foundation refunds and safe footprint exits

Owner: recovery worker, integrated and accepted by the branch-completion coordinator. Base: `df03d41b`, checkout `unbuilt-foundations`. Implementation is pending final integration, independent review and the combined gate; no recovery commit or main merge is claimed here.

## Source and scope

[AoE2 DE discussion 137076](https://forums.ageofempires.com/t/lose-resources-for-deleting-building-foundation/137076) contains direct player reports that deleting a foundation with no work returns its cost; a reply distinguishes the proportional refund after construction starts. This is community observation on the official forum, not a developer specification or a fresh DE play measurement. Search result 86585 was rejected after opening it: it belongs to AoE I DE. No installed-game measurement or official patch explaining this refund was obtained.

The coordinator explicitly approved the narrow M2 boundary: Delete refunds only a foundation with no build progress. Started and complete buildings keep their current no-refund behavior. A construction state saves no paid-cost receipt, so new and old foundations use `ownerConstructionCost` at execution, as blocked-ground cancellation already does. The actual refund can differ from the original payment if a civilization, age or team bonus changed between placement and Delete. No save field or migration is added. The handler rechecks current ownership, and combat destruction does not pass through this refund path.

## Reproduction and implementation

The first eight `deleteFoundationRefund.test.ts` cases ran before the implementation: five failed on missing refunds and three boundary controls passed. The voluntary Delete handler now credits the owner's stockpile and marks the resource slot dirty before the existing destroy operation. Tests include plain and Malian Houses, the current Briton Town Center price before and after its Castle discount, schema-1 and schema-2 saves with no paid receipt, started and finished buildings, refused enemy deletion, changed ownership before execution, duplicate queued Deletes and exact replay of a recorded refund. The civ/age changes are staged save conditions, not a claim that a normal match lets a player change civilization or lose an age.

The integration reviewer separately found IR1: footprint exits were planned against unchanged occupancy. A villager and a sheep, or two sheep, chose the same exit, and the later resource claim could strand the earlier unit. Seven added mechanism cases cover competing exclusive destinations, distinct successful exits, the sixteen shared unit slots and an exit already occupied by a unit. Five of the first six cases failed before the fix; the fourteen-unit positive control already passed. The fix reserves destinations during planning, permits units to share only the remaining subcell slots, and requires an animal's cell to contain no existing or planned unit or animal. It performs no relocation unless every destination was found.

These exit cases drive the real foundation-start and occupancy mechanisms on a 10×10 synthetic world, checking positions, final claims and passability of the first relocated occupant. They do not drive page controls or play a saved match through that particular exit. Reservations are local to one `moveOff` call and are never saved: a refused plan changes no position or occupancy, while a successful plan changes them together through the existing relocation callback. No new replay or save state is introduced by IR1.
