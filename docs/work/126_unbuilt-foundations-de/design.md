# Unbuilt foundations in Definitive Edition: the sources and the choices (M1 and M2)

Research for [the plan](plan.md), done 2026-10-14 by the M1 worker. The plan asked which units may cross an unbuilt foundation, what happens to units on it when construction starts, and whether another player may build over one and what happens when both start. Where the sources left a choice, the coordinator chose on 2026-10-14; each choice is marked as this game's.

## The choices the sources left, and the coordinator's decisions

1. **An enemy's unit on the footprint at the start.** (A) moved off like any unit: forum 287158 saw a villager "made space for the enemy's Castle foundation", which the reporter knew "only ... from friendly building foundations", in a report answered "This is already tracked". (B) it holds the start until it leaves: the same wiki paragraph has "Buildings can be constructed if there is no enemy building or unit present at the location of building foundation"; 87863 scopes its fix to own and allied units; players describe current DE so (forum 278625, 2025: "villagers would stop building if sth would stand on the foundation. (like how they do now when they start)"; forum 290106, 2026-10-09: "allow the vills to continue building up to that point even if a unit is on top"). **Decision: (B).** The builders wait beside the site, with no cancel; an AI's builders wait the same way.
2. **An ally's unstarted foundation and another player's placement.** 185872 says "players" with no qualifier, and every report is about an enemy's foundation the placer could not see. **Decision: only an enemy may build over one.** What the player is shown blocks it, and allies see each other's foundations.
3. **When the losing foundation of an overlap goes.** When its own builders arrive (45185, 46295), or the moment the other starts (forum 278625, 2025: one player's farm "automatically" deleted "when he starts building the wall"). **Decision: when its builders arrive**, refunded in full at the owner's price.
4. **Defaults the coordinator accepted or set.** The start check runs for every seat, since an AI's site may now overlap the human's. Living animals on the footprint (Gaia herdables, wild animals, the owner's own) are moved off like the owner's units, after the wiki sentence; carcasses and static resources still cancel. An ally's animal is moved as its units are, and an enemy's sheep holds the start as its units do, by the same wiki paragraph. Animals do not cross an unstarted foundation, a stated bound with no source either way (superseded after review round 0, below). Placing over a unit the player can see stays refused, outside M1.

## How this game moves a unit off

DE walks a unit off the footprint. Here it is put, in the tick the building starts, on the nearest open cell outside the footprint by its own passability, counted in 4-connected steps across the footprint with east, west, south, north breaking ties, and it keeps its orders. It is all or none: if one has nowhere to go, nobody moves and the start waits. A walk-off would need a footprint that is open to the units on it and closed to everyone else for the few ticks they take to leave, and a stream of units crossing could hold the start indefinitely; the cell it lands on is always beside the footprint, so the jump is never longer than the footprint is wide. This is a known difference from DE and is in spec §8.1.

## Changed after review round 0

The coordinator's read-only review of the phase-1 branch (`reviews/0_implementation.md`) led to four rule decisions on 2026-10-14, each in spec §8.1:

- **A builder of its own seat's site does not hold another seat's start.** Two seats' builders could each stand on the other's unstarted site and both wait forever. Such a unit is moved off like the owner's units. The approach to a site does not avoid other seats' unstarted foundations, which would show the human where a hidden enemy foundation lies. Narrowed in review round 1 (below).
- **At the start, any other seat's unstarted foundation stands aside, an ally's as well as an enemy's.** Placement is unchanged: no seat places over an own or allied foundation it can know about, so allies overlap only through the fog, and then the first to start wins.
- **Animals cross an unstarted foundation as units do**, since in DE a sheep is a unit; keeping them off showed where a hidden foundation lay. A relic spilled onto one lies there as on open ground, and the start cancels for it.
- **A site a builder standing on a future wall can reach must be reached from every side it could then be on**, not from any one of them.

## Changed after review round 1

The same reviewer's focused re-review (`reviews/1_implementation.md`) found the round-0 builder exception too wide, and the coordinator narrowed it on 2026-10-15, in spec §8.1:

- **Only a builder standing beside its own site is excused.** A unit whose order is building its own seat's site holds no other seat's start only while it stands on a cell beside that site's footprint, where it builds from. One passing through on its way to a site elsewhere holds the start like any enemy's unit: moved off, it could land on the far side of a pass the foundation then seals and never reach its own site.

## M2 recovery, 2026-10-17

The recovery coordinator approved the existing plan's fallback rather than adding saved paid-cost receipts: Delete of a foundation with no work refunds its owner's current effective construction price. No refund is added after construction starts or for a completed building. Direct player observations in [AoE2 DE discussion 137076](https://forums.ageofempires.com/t/lose-resources-for-deleting-building-foundation/137076) support the zero-progress refund, while also describing DE's proportional partial-construction refund, which is outside this milestone. The source is community evidence, not an official statement or a game measurement. Discussion 86585 was rejected because it belongs to AoE I DE.

[Recovery notes](snapshots/recovery-2026-10-17.md) hold the current-price save bound, tests and integration review IR1's additional requirement that footprint occupants reserve compatible destinations before anyone moves. The reservation exists only during that clearing call; it adds no saved state.
