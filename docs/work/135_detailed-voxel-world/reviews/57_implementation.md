## Reports

RED as a reference match. M21 materially improves the neck's curve and taper over M20's block stack, but the head still misses the reference's dominant form. The subject reads as an emerging head and neck, without torso or limbs.

## Findings and disposition

1. **Head remains the primary defect.** In matched and caster-close, tall upright horn/ear shapes dominate a small skull. The long narrow snout and tiny shelf jaw read as a beaked creature. The reference has a broad, low skull, substantial cheek/brow and jaw masses, and a rearward horn silhouette. Reblock those masses before adding surface detail.
2. **Neck gains curvature but loses clear articulation.** Caster-side and opposite-side show a more convincing taper, yet large wedge plates dominate; four unequal sections and the short partly hidden upper transition do not read clearly. Keep the curve, clarify three changes of direction, and let the skull overlap the short upper section.
3. **Flame and pad remain coarse.** Detail, overhead and spell-end expose a huge square-lobed flame with broad flat faces. The pad is an orange field with isolated white pools, rather than the reference's interlocking central light. Break the flame silhouette into smaller irregular lobes; reconnect the pad's light through its centre after the head is right.
