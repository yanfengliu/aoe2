### Distinct mechanism and evidence

A concrete alternative is to author each editable component as a complete cubical cell complex before sampling. Author closed two-dimensional contours across successive fixed-grid depth slices, with independent near/far taper and multiple intervals where a mouth or recess needs them. The entire intended volume, material boundary and root are frozen together. Consecutive slices must share actual cells, their in-slice sets must be face-connected, and the complex must avoid edge-only/corner-only boundary contacts. These are construction preconditions, not a later repair operation.

Extract only the complex's exposed faces, triangulate its planar quads and pass that complete closed surface through the unchanged fixed-center sampler. Every center is strictly inside its own unit cube and at least half a cell from the cubical boundary. Sampling therefore reproduces exactly the authored set. Connected slice sets with shared cells give face-connected components by construction. A closed two-manifold boundary still needs a separate topology check; six-connectivity alone is insufficient.

This changes the construction from predicting sampled continuity from oblique facets to authoring the final volumetric boundary at the sink's own resolution. It does not require imported art, raster tracing, a new renderer, relaxed checks, filtering islands or cell filler after sampling. A future implementation must reject any attempt to convert a failed sampled map and patch its islands under this name. No such conversion or actual component was built here.

The unrelated seven-cell stair samples exactly as seven connected cells. A held-out 4-by-4 hollow contour samples exactly as its twelve perimeter cells and preserves four empty interior cells. Both have closed consistently oriented boundary meshes and classifier agreement. A diagonal-only two-cube negative remains two components and has a nonmanifold shared edge; the proposed preconditions reject it. This demonstrates a bounded structural mechanism and its topology hazard, not dragon art.

This family is not proven artistically superior to M19/M20. Their run language is already expressive enough to encode arbitrary occupancy. The inspected M20 source instead expands records across rectangular height/depth ranges; the three native images show horizontal bright ledges, a column-like rear and near-parallel crest rails. The proposed distinction must be actual independent contour authorship across depth, not a new serializer for those old rows. Merely deriving a mesh from the old occupancy would repeat that failed construction.
