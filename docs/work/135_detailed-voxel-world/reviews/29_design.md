### Actual observations

- The frozen reference organizes its head and emergence mass through short intersecting angular red/gold/cream forms, an uneven swept crest and broken bright passages. M15 matched/detail instead emphasize a broad smooth crown, very long crescent jaw, large mouth gap and tall nearly vertical neck. Side/rear views expose extensive uninterrupted surfaces and repeated horizontal boundaries. Fine stair steps do not change those dominant relationships.
- M15's jaw visibly returns into the head, the mouth is open, and side/rear/overhead views show a continuous grounded mass rather than paired boots or a humanoid limb chain. This supports preservation of the corrected subject. Its pedestal-like throat remains a fidelity problem, not evidence that the humanoid returned.
- The flame has a swollen rounded envelope, broad axial color regions and a few large white islands. Opposite-side/rear/overhead show how much of that envelope remains plain. The pad's broad bright strips and repeated rectangular patches form a coarse lattice. Both compete with the head more than the smaller broken reference groups do. No clipping finding is made; the corrected last-two-column background evidence in Review 28 is retained.

### Inferences and recommendations

**The overlooked risk is relational composition, not insufficient cell detail.** A more angular dome atop the same tall neck, with the same long jaw and dominant flame, could satisfy the construction wording while retaining the wrong image balance. Require M16's fixed matched view to visibly improve crest-to-muzzle direction, jaw-to-skull proportion, neck-to-ground transition and head/flame/pad dominance together. These are visual relationships, not numerical acceptance ratios. Inspect side and rear for the same structural improvement, then judge material rhythm. Keep one implementer; no additional sculpting system or renderer branch is justified by these pixels.

Explicit 3D material ownership addresses swallowed bright passages only when it produces connected, directional hot regions with broken surfaces and unequal tips. “Smaller flame” alone can yield a miniature version of the same rounded bulb. Similarly, unequal pad patches must change its overall warm mosaic and broken perimeter, rather than jitter the existing white lattice. Keep all frozen controls and judge actual native pixels; the predeclared ROI only tests confinement.

**Subject negative control:** unchanged M13 matched/front show separate flat-toed boots beneath thighs/pelvis, a narrow waist/torso, and a bent forearm ending in a palm/finger cluster below the mouth. Opposite/rear expose two discrete ground contacts and the arm chain separate from the jaw. Those violate the current subject criteria: **REJECT**. Overhead compresses those cues and cannot decide alone. The same five-view manual check rejects this known labelled historical defect and accepts M15's bounded subject correction. It establishes neither blinded/general oracle sensitivity nor automated detection. Direct display plus an explicit anatomy judgment matters; multiple angles alone previously existed and failed through interpretation.

**Orchestration:** CURRENT lagged M15/M16 during inspection; root reports its documentation owner is actively reconciling that checkpoint. Preserve subject PASS/full-art REJECT/candidate pending distinctions. This is an acknowledged active documentation fix, not a product failure. These pixels establish no engine limitation or reason to relax the reference.
