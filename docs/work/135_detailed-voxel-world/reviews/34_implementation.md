### Independent native verdict

**Subject category: PASS. Early head major-form gate: REJECT. Full reference fidelity: REJECT; acceptance remains OPEN.** The early rejection is about large forms already visible at this coarse stage, not missing eyes, teeth or decorative treatment.

**Bounded subject and volume gains.** Matched/front/opposite show an upper head and attached lower jaw with a real mouth opening. Rear/opposite show one continuous bent neck/root reaching the pad, without a pelvis, paired boots or a separate reaching arm/hand. Overhead establishes visible cranial depth; it does not independently settle lower anatomy. Close/side and low-up corroborate the connected head/jaw/root reading. The huge white eye panel and stack of cuboid cheek blocks are absent. These are useful gains and preserve the corrected subject.

**F11 remains material at the early major-form scale.** In matched/detail/caster-close, one very large golden roof-like wedge runs from a high rear edge to a long tapered snout. Its broad sloping top subsumes the crest, cranial bulk and muzzle into one dominant envelope. The reference instead separates a swept jagged upper crest from shorter intersecting head planes. Overhead shows a broad, largely rectangular taper rather than clearly distinct coarse skull/crest masses. A coarse version can omit surface ornament while still establishing those relationships; they are not yet established here.

Caster-side/opposite-side expose two long near-parallel jaw shelves with a wide horizontal gap. A real opening is present, but the cheek/hinge region is not localized as a distinct structural mass; it reads as the rear joining edge of the two shelves. Matched/front retain that generic long-snouted profile. This is a major-volume issue, not a request to add teeth around the gap.

Rear-oblique/low-up/exploratory show a broad plain red back and a neck with nearly uniform width that bends before meeting a small planted ground contact. The bend is a gain, but bending a largely continuous support has not supplied the irregular emergence transition or the reference-specific head-to-root balance. The long roof above the plain stem still dominates the composition. The five required views therefore do not pass the fixed early discriminator. A construction label, shared-boundary claim or occupancy count would not change this visual finding.

**F12/F13 remain rejected controls, separate from the early-head decision.** Matched/front/spell-end retain the broad squared warm flame lobes and small white window-like groups; opposite/rear show its plain sides. Matched/overhead retain the mostly uniform warm pad with separated white islands and a broken red edge. Those are the known M16 fidelity gaps. They neither supply a head pass nor cause the early-head rejection. This round makes no new exact object-pixel-identity or clipping claim.
