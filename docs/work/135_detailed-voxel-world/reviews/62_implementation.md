## Findings and disposition

Subject category PASS: an open dragon head emerges directly through a continuous neck; there are no humanoid limbs or torso. Rear, opposite-side and low-up views establish visible volume and an attached jaw. Small backward crests remain subordinate.

Head/neck art REJECT. Matched, detail and caster-close show long parallel terraces dominating the entire skull roof and lower jaw. The resulting broad beak and thin jaw shelf lack the reference's forceful brow, deep cheek and compact bent hinge. The eye is a small white slit on a large plain red side wall; the roof carries more visual structure than the face. Caster-side exposes the shelf-like jaw and sparse rectangular teeth particularly clearly.

Four connected neck regions are readable, but caster-side, reverse and exploration show a repeated wedge/waist rhythm, with broad red faces, triangular yellow fields and stippled seams. These become the anatomy rather than supporting it. The back remains a largely plain red zigzag column. Continuity and count do not establish the intended unequal, sculpted region hierarchy or convincing short upper transition.

The material/value organization remains weak: large yellow roof planes and white teeth are legible, but broad red side/back faces suppress cheek and joint depth. The reference has richer, purposeful plate and voxel relief.
