## Findings and disposition

The done condition is appropriately coarse. Four unequal masses and three shape joints can be judged without individual scales. Skull volume, muzzle direction, cheek location, jaw attachment and crest sweep are large relationships. Rejecting M19/M20 for these does not silently demand deferred detail. Exact hidden plate count or exact unseen anatomy would exceed the evidence; coherent authored depth remains sufficient.

Freeze the integer sink, public meshing path, stable overlap-color resolution and real mouth-depth requirement. Preserve exact M16 flame, pad, staff and sparks as controls, with their fidelity still unaccepted. Preserve M20's clearer continuity as comparative evidence. No complete neck or head component earns a geometry freeze as accepted art.

Report 37's direct-run family has now received two native attempts. Its warning about different row endpoints across depth remains relevant, but another global box-row correction would repeat the same difficult manual encoding. Original 36's independently shaped local surfaces remain underexplored. M16's world-coordinate clipped solids do not test that assembly discipline: broad root/crown masses already fill the relationships being sought.

Propose one bounded local-surface assembly experiment, if root authorizes it. Use original closed polyhedra per component, with independently authored vertices, faces and local frames; sample the finished solids once at world-cell centers. Local coordinates alone add no expressive power. The materially different construction must be new section boundaries and restricted junctions, not matrices wrapped around old rows, rings or M16 solids.

Before building, freeze a component worksheet with each axis, eccentric near/far cross-sections, taper, throat patch, overlap socket and exposed shoulder. Construct the four neck bodies pairwise from ground upward. Each neighboring pair overlaps internally while an oblique shoulder continues onto its side/back; no tube or global backing supplies continuity. Keep the upper transition shorter. Give skull, brow, descending muzzle, localized cheek and short jaw separate volumes; vary crest blade direction, depth and taper. Reserve mouth air before joining the cheek and jaw. Build relationships before decoration.

Then union the independently shaped parts and run one unchanged eleven-view batch. The discriminator is simultaneous four-section rhythm and cranial hierarchy in matched/detail, with side, opposite, rear, overhead and low views preserving actual depth and junctions. Reject lost shoulders after union, globally horizontal ledges, parallel crest rails, broad cheek backing, disconnected parts or a favorable single projection. Component worksheets, connectivity and counts corroborate construction only. This proposal does not authorize a model or reset aggregate attempts.
