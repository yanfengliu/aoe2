## Findings and disposition

1. **HOLD: neck planes remain diagrammatic.** Caster-side and caster-close show a regular diagonal striped upper section above broad, almost featureless trapezoidal sidewalls. Low-up, rear-oblique, opposite-side, and exploration reveal the same stacked block construction around the neck. The surface treatment changes from dots to plain fill to parallel ribs without explaining a coherent thick, faceted mass. The reference uses unequal chunky plane changes and irregular joints that turn through the volume; M24 still reads as a bent stack of cut blocks.
2. **HOLD: head planes are overly regular.** Detail and caster-close expose the crown as many shallow parallel terraces, while the rear skull and two muzzle bars retain long straight box faces. The skull is recognizable, but the repetitive steps substitute for a small number of deliberate, unequal crown, brow, cheek, and muzzle facets. This is especially apparent beside the reference's interlocking angular highlights and thicker broken contour.
3. **Full artwork remains on hold.** The flame still reads as a rounded white-centered bulb and the pad as a flat tiled sheet. These known control defects are outside this bounded correction and are not accepted here.
