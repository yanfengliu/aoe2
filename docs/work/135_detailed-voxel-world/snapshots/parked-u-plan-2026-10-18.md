## Problem and outcome

The owner wants researched, refined voxel art throughout the game: environment and characters as well as buildings. Trees, animals and ore nodes are explicitly criticized as bulky. Deliver smaller purposeful forms, recognizable anatomy and equipment, material separation and richer natural structure at normal play zoom.

## Scope

Trees, vegetation, rocks/ores, animals/mounts, terrain/shore props and human unit families. The tested building/villager increment remains work134. World implementation follows its stable rendering interfaces; UI136 is independent. Original procedural assets remain required. Root owns integration, canonical spec, gallery and gates; voxel_world_direction owns the fresh reference/source audit in detailed-world-1017 from main1dc71788.

## Approach

Study primary voxel artists and game studios with about8–12 relevant image references across environment, animals and characters. Record observation versus inference, creator/source/date/hash and offline versus realtime limits. Keep reference images ignored. Agree concrete silhouettes, material/value hierarchy, purposeful voxel scale, natural variation, animation and picking contracts before implementation. Prefer compact reusable meshes replacing existing masses; inspect holes against the actual input proxy. Preserve public caps unless an evidenced contract change is explicitly accepted.

## Acceptance criteria

- Primary references produce a coherent original art grammar and asset-specific details, covering the whole requested class.
- Trees, ore/rocks, animals/mounts and representative human families improve visibly at normal and close zoom in both styles; native before/after/diffs and independent judgment substantiate it.
- Picking, fog/memory, footprints, anatomy/rigs, tool reach, saves and gameplay remain correct; meaningful tests and actual input cover affected behavior.
- Resource/part/triangle/batch costs remain bounded and representative live performance is measured; original software gap remains openly tracked.
- Full gates, independent integrated review, main merge/push/hosted checks and owned-resource cleanup complete.
