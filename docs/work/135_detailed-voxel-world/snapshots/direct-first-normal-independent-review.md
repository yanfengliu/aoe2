## What the references establish

Town to City T03 uses compact, sometimes nearly solid cypress crowns; it does **not** establish that every successful tree needs exposed branches or large holes. Its foliage has a controlled overall taper, broken vertical/internal facets and deliberate local value grouping. Repeating fine horizontal contour lines does not produce that relationship. Teardown Z02’s sparse trees show legible trunk-to-canopy divisions and uneven branch spans, while its buildings distinguish large planes from selected fine openings and trim. It is an unmatched cinematic scene, not a measured RTS comparison.

Voxelnauts Z12 is a humanoid/effect study, not a conifer template: its bent body, hand/staff relationship and directed effect illustrate meaningful structure. Latica L1 is an architectural landscape, not botanical measurement: repeated columns and terraces support larger masses, voids and paths. These sources support a qualitative demand for authored hierarchy across the world. They supply no exact dimensions, voxel budget, material recipe, hidden pipeline, runtime cost or guaranteed construction algorithm for our tree.
