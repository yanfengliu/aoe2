## Attempt boundary and a distinct route

M01–M03 combine world-axis polygon extrusions, surface panels and ellipsoidal fire lobes. M04–M05 retain that family while moving the character to view-derived profile solids; M06 also moves fire to profile layers and adds character Lambert shading. Native M01/M03/M05/M06 comparisons show better placement, a broader pool and more frontal ornament, but not a transition to fully articulated sculpture. This closes only the proposition that those inspected repairs already achieved the fixed quality bar. It does not prove that all procedural construction, all voxels or this renderer are incapable. Historical elapsed authoring/compute cost is unknown; this review made zero render attempts.

A materially different mechanism from M06 is **semantic volume authoring in each part's own spatial axes**: establish shoulder/elbow/wrist and hip/boot positions; define independently varying width and depth sections for the torso, cheek, muzzle and jaw; carve actual separations; attach armor pieces around those volumes; then add selected bevels, cavities and inlays on the surfaces they belong to. Fire would be several deliberately authored, tapering and diverging spatial streams with unequal protruding hot tongues, rather than a stack of silhouette/color caps. The remaining art work is choosing those original sections, overlaps, voids and heat groupings so they express this particular reference at matched size. A new tool or the word “volumetric” does not solve that work.

The coordinator subsequently confirmed direct human authorization for continuation after M06 and an existing owner working on articulated 3D, detail, flame, shading and multiple angles. Therefore this route description is a discriminator for that ongoing work, **not a request to dispatch another implementer or seek renewed allowance**. M07–M09 filenames alone are no evidence that the mechanism or quality succeeded.
