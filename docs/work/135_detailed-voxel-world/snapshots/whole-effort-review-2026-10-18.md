## Findings

1. **The unresolved objective is a coherent family.** Teardown's structural layers and quiet fields, Latica's larger terrace/column/edge hierarchy, Town to City's ordinary props and openings, and Voxelnauts' joint/pose/grip construction are different expressions of deliberate authored relationships. The current actual game and engine specimens still expose their recipes: ribbed crowns/rocks, simple bodies and stamped contacts. More conifer trials cannot establish animal anatomy, mineral-bearing host stone, believable tool contact, or a coherent bank/ground scale. The existing research already identifies this; no further broad research is needed.

2. **S3 is materially different, but narrow.** Finite face-connected fork volumes and nonvanishing tips address S2's diagonal singleton and its depth-filled cuts. Preserving mass, old guards and budgets is sensible. Three dominant fork groups can support a useful hierarchy. However, their prescribed separated height bands can also become three shelves, and integer volumes can still become chunky blocks. Passing the two projected cleft cases proves those minima only. Frozen palette/light/ground mean S3 cannot resolve the full scene's material/contact problems. Its first Normal image must still decide whether this is a tree with connected asymmetric boughs, rather than a cone, sparse pads or shelves. There is no rendered S2 or S3 art gain to accept.

3. **The loop risk is oscillation between mass and gaps.** D/H/S1/S2 history alternates regular crown, sparse pads, filled cone and mechanically insufficient cuts. Those failures are useful, but new predicates must not become the design brief. Keep the present safety/contour guards; do not add another abstract shape score. The good orchestration change was stopping S1 after one failed native view. Repeat that economy for S3, then broaden only after a visible gain. A failed first look does not need eight views, full release gates or another capture framework. Correctness gates and owned-process cleanup remain necessary; source seals and test passes are not evidence of craft.

4. **The engine proof answers a smaller question than requested.** The inspected Normal/close public-runtime images show that fine indexed geometry renders. They also visibly fail art quality. Matching subject classes and physical bounds while authoring a second set of models does not isolate the adapter or demonstrate the same artwork. No missing engine feature follows. Freeze the same geometry, materials, transforms, camera, light and scale in the public-runtime and actual-game paths for a causal comparison. If both look poor, change the art; if the public path succeeds and the game path fails, investigate the adapter/rendering difference. A real admission/rendering failure on the same valid subject would provide a concrete engine issue.
