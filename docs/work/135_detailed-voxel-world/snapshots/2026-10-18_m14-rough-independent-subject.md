## Visible evidence

- **Matched/front-oblique:** one cranial mass, projecting upper muzzle, eye, tooth and open lower jaw sit on a single substantial emergence volume. There is no waist, shoulder/elbow/wrist chain, hand, split lower body or paired boot contact. The lower projection reads as a jaw with teeth rather than a reaching arm.
- **Opposite-side/caster-side:** the mouth opening is actual empty space between upper muzzle and lower jaw; the lower jaw returns into the rear head/throat. It is not a colored line on a solid face. These views provide the clearest jaw evidence where the matched view foreshortens it.
- **Rear-oblique:** the skull has substantial depth and a continuous back flowing into one broad neck/base. There is no humanoid silhouette on the reverse. The mouth need not be exposed from every rear angle; here its side opening remains partly visible.
- **Overhead:** the skull is a broad, tapering volume rather than a thin frontal plaque. Its rear merges into a substantial single base within the glowing ground patch. There are no separated foot islands.
- **Low-up/exploratory:** the same broad throat continues to the ground without an unsupported gap or narrow separate stand. The model reads as a thick emerging neck/head. The flame naturally hides some mouth content in the spell-end view; that view alone would not establish the jaw.

The tall, blank and somewhat columnar throat remains a visible rough-construction limitation. It is broad and continuous enough that I do not read it as a small head mounted on a narrow pillar, so it does not block this limited subject pass. No detailed dragon anatomy, convincing ground eruption, reference-specific crest/scute rhythm, fine material grouping, flame or pad fidelity is accepted here. The rough model is visibly far from final reference quality.
