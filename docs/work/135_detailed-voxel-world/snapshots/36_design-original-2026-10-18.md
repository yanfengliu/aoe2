## Done condition and disqualifiers before implementation

The finite early test is component construction, not unlimited fine detail. At matched size, root and a fresh reviewer must identify four unequal neck sections with three visible shape joints along one continuous curved neck; a distinct swept crest, compact skull/brow, short muzzle, localized cheek/hinge and lower jaw; and the head-to-neck balance of the reference. At side, opposite, rear and overhead, those components must remain connected volumes with coherent overlaps, a real mouth opening and no broad backing that erases the distinctions. Inspect all eleven actual originals individually; obscured views supply less evidence, not a pass. Use the maintained before/after diff with the intended head/neck region frozen before capture.

At this gate, fine teeth, individual tiny scales, spark shape, flame motion and exact far-side plate count are deferred. A small placeholder facial accent may establish face orientation, but bright eye decoration cannot repair the skull. Flame and pad remain rejected controls; an early head/neck pass cannot claim full-reference or game-art acceptance.

Reject a smooth stem with painted joint lines; four equal stacked boxes; renamed rings with the same global bridge topology; a roof with crest detail pasted on top; overlaps that erase the three joints; disconnected collars hidden by the matched camera; jaw interpreted as an arm; humanoid hands or paired feet; a favorable camera/light change; or voxel, triangle, component and watertightness counts offered instead of the visible result. Reject changes outside the frozen experiment region or controls. Preserve useful bounded gains without promoting them to a full-art pass.
