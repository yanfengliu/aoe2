# Unit attack animation

## Goal

Every perspective-eligible successful unit attack whose attacker remains currently visible must produce an immediate, smooth, role-readable rigid-part strike for that attacker. A group order may animate several villagers independently on the same boar tick while approaching villagers keep their locomotion pose. The presentation must not change damage, reloads, pathing, roots, selection, or hit authority.

## Contract

The simulation records one bounded latest successful-hit event per attacker entity reference with the impact tick, target root, and captured witnesses. The first successful impact each tick and every later impact after a player-command LOS fingerprint mutation synchronize visibility sources; unchanged same-tick impacts and fine-only movement reuse that proven-current snapshot, and source fingerprints avoid unnecessary `VisibilityMap` recomputation. Recursive building destruction compares the actual source fingerprints of the building plus its bounded garrison IDs; construction invalidates only when completion actually adds a source. A player, including the attacker's owner, is a witness only when at least one cell of the attacker footprint and at least one cell of the target footprint are visible to that perspective at that impact. If no perspective qualifies, no event or coordinates are recorded, so retained ranged orders cannot leak hidden target coordinates. Projection exposes only fresh witnessed events that have not been suppressed for that perspective. Every tower targets from one immutable pass-start visibility snapshot; after the complete successful pass, final LOS refreshes exactly once if any unit died and before output suppression/checkpoint publication. The first actual root change records a cancellation tick: movement-facing becomes authoritative immediately while strike and ambient-suppression weights smoothstep to zero across one observable tick. Presented roots plus attack phase, weight, and ambient suppression remain deterministic and continuous across the boundary; fresh disposable gait history may restart and is not a warm/fresh equivalence contract. Equal display time freezes exactly. The renderer submits one monotonic animation clock driven only by positive simulation-display deltas, so pause repeats the same Voxel timestamp and replay rewinds rebase without violating Voxel's no-backward-time contract. The event tick is the visible impact pose, followed by recovery, because the current simulation has no pre-hit wind-up event. Coincident presented roots still animate: a warm renderer retains its prior finite displayed facing and a fresh renderer uses the role's authored forward.

Humanoid tools, swords, bows, cavalry weapons, rams, and throwing siege mechanisms transform as connected rigid assemblies around shared joints. Feet, wheels, roots, contact shadows, and the presented silhouette authority remain planted. All Voxel harmonic ambient motion follows the injected monotonic display-progress clock and freezes while the simulation is paused. Monks remain non-attacking: validation and the direct helper reject new attacks, and the execution boundary clears any hydrated or persisted Monk attack before damage or event emission. Wildlife retaliation, gathering clips, projectile travel, skeletal animation, and root motion are outside this slice.

The feed is render-only and excluded from ordinary user saves. Hydration examines only the final 1,024 candidates, validates and canonicalizes fresh in-bounds records, and retains the latest record per attacker; after forced bootstrap publication, recorder state changes only on add, change, suppression, or expiry. When a witnessed attacker leaves one player's current view, the feed persists that player in `suppressedFor` before recorder checkpoint publication. New recording snapshots therefore restore the same event, per-perspective hide-once state, and exact source/target coordinates, attack-channel phase, weight, and cancellation state without changing deterministic gameplay state; historical checkpoints whose feed predates `suppressedFor` cannot reconstruct an earlier hide/reveal. Playback establishes its frame clock before consuming elapsed time, so entering replay does not synthesize a tick. A fresh renderer may reinitialize disposable gait history, so full assembled rig matrix equality is not promised across replay reset. Raw projection retains fogged live entities for later reveal, but the bridge filters the final perspective, prior-position interpolation requires visibility in both the prior and current frames, and `RenderStore` keeps a local cue tombstone as defense in depth. Captured witnesses prevent later vision from disclosing an impact the player did not witness.

## Evidence

Unit tests cover event recording/filtering/expiry/save/replay, mutation-aware same-tick visibility, max-pop alternating fine-move/impact scan reuse, coarse-cell invalidation, bounded building/garrison invalidation, construction-source signaling, hidden ranged target admission, immutable tower-pass targeting with one post-kill refresh, tower-kill final-LOS suppression, checkpoint-persisted per-perspective suppression, fresh replay projection, simultaneous villagers, impact timing, coincident-root prior/fresh facing, production cancellation-boundary attack-channel equivalence plus fresh gait restart, role poses, connected weapon assemblies, attack/locomotion precedence, pause/rewind behavior, intentionally disposable gait history, fog reveal confidentiality, and base-recipe/hit-proxy matrix parity. A headless browser test checks accepted/presented base recipe matrices for concurrent attackers, moving non-attackers, and impact-time change. The renderer seam separately proves that repeated paused wall-clock frames submit the exact same monotonic Voxel animation timestamp, including across a backward replay rebase; the browser matrix diagnostic is pre-harmonic and is not claimed as GPU harmonic-matrix evidence. Fixed before/after captures and a pixel diff isolate the authored impact delta on the attackers.
