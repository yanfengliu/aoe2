## Reports

### Codex (`gpt-6-astra`)

The report whole, redacted only of its absolute paths.

1. **R4-1 — P1: Five unique technologies promise bonuses that existing units do not receive.** Copy: `src/ui/hud/helpText/upgradeHelp.ts:74`, lines 74, 77–78, 86 and 88: `carrack`, `el-dorado`, `farimba`, `recurve-bow`, `shatagni`. The research-completion switch never applies these five through the existing-unit updater; its unique-effect cases end at technologyOps.ts:399–415 (`src/game/simulation/bridge/technologyOps.ts:399`). Newly created units do receive them through combatStateFactory.ts:227–235 (`src/game/simulation/bridge/combatStateFactory.ts:227`). **Correct fact:** these bonuses currently affect units created or rebuilt after research; completing research does not grant them to the army already present.

2. **R4-2 — P1: The Transport Ship’s advertised boarding click does not work through the normal entity-click route.** Copy: `src/ui/hud/helpText/unitHelp.ts:69`, “Right-click it with land units to board them.” A click hitting the ship submits the entity command and returns before the ground fallback (voxelSelectionController.ts:325–343 (`src/input/voxelSelectionController.ts:325`)). That router has no boarding branch: an owned ship leads to repair when applicable, otherwise movement (contextAtEntityRouter.ts:62–79 (`src/game/simulation/bridge/contextAtEntityRouter.ts:62`), line 148). Boarding exists in the separate cell router (contextRouter.ts:114–117 (`src/game/simulation/bridge/contextRouter.ts:114`)). **Correct fact:** the cell route can board units; right-clicking the ship entity does not reliably implement the advertised control. The repaired near-shore unloading description itself agrees with the code.

3. **R4-3 — P2: Siege Engineers overstates what its percentages multiply.** Copy: `src/ui/hud/helpText/technologyHelp.ts:20`, “+20% attack vs. buildings … +40%.” The multipliers affect particular building-class **bonus attacks**, leaving base attack untouched (buildingAttack.ts:131–139 (`src/game/simulation/buildingAttack.ts:131`), lines 168–183). For example, a Mangonel’s base 40 stays 40, while its building bonus rises from 35 to 42—not a 20% increase to the combined attack (`src/game/simulation/prototypeUnitRules/statTables.ts:140`, `src/game/simulation/buildingAttack.ts:72`). **Correct fact:** say “bonus attack vs. buildings”; the +1 siege range claim is correct.

4. **R4-4 — P2: Wheelbarrow and Hand Cart omit Fishing Ships from their carrying bonuses.** Copy: technologyHelp.ts:54–55 (`src/ui/hud/helpText/technologyHelp.ts:54`). Fishing Ships run the same economy loop (villagerEconomySystem.ts:234–244 (`src/game/simulation/bridge/systems/villagerEconomySystem.ts:234`)), and its carrying calculation applies these multipliers without a villager restriction (gatheringStep.ts:164–170 (`src/game/simulation/bridge/systems/gatheringStep.ts:164`); economyTechEffects.ts:116–151 (`src/game/simulation/economyTechEffects.ts:116`)). **Correct fact:** Villagers **and Fishing Ships** gain the carrying multipliers; only Villagers receive these technologies’ movement bonuses.

5. **R4-5 — P2: Treadmill Crane also speeds Fishing Ship construction.** Copy: `src/ui/hud/helpText/technologyHelp.ts:91`, “Villagers build 20% faster.” Fishing Ships receive ordinary build commands for Fish Traps (constructionStartOps.ts:58–71 (`src/game/simulation/bridge/constructionStartOps.ts:58`), lines 124–129). 6. **R4-6 — P2: Yeomen’s named unit set omits Janissaries.** Copy: `src/ui/hud/helpText/upgradeHelp.ts:92`, “Foot archers and Skirmishers +1 range.” Its actual effect additionally includes `janissary` and `elite-janissary` (uniqueTechnologies.ts:100–106 (`src/game/simulation/uniqueTechnologies.ts:100`), lines 241–247). 7. **R4-7 — P2: `statedAsMade` silently skips consecutive production claims.** The repaired Anarchy copy is at `src/ui/hud/helpText/upgradeHelp.ts:69`; the remaining defect is in `tests/ui/helpTextRequirements.test.ts:88`. That is the entity route's defect, outside this work; the line now says what the ship is for and the two steps to unload, and DE's "right-click the Transport Ship to load" returns when the route is mended. Listed for plan 106.
