### Independent Astra reviewer: diagnosis and governing contract

**The proposed split is justified by the recorded trajectory and the existing contract.** The original test explicitly says its target is whether a Monk carrying a relic can walk it home, not the AI's knowledge of relics (`tests/simulation/aiRelicsSelfPlay.test.ts:17–22`). Its header also says exact delivery ticks and the winner are not asserted, and that a trajectory preventing one seat from holding all relics requires re-deriving the case from the ledger (`:24–33`). Its unconditional countdown and empty-resource assertions (`:93–103`) depend on a stronger strategic outcome than its stated defect mechanism.

The specification requires an AI collector to fetch known, reachable relics on its own or shared explored ground; it expressly rejects access to never-seen relic locations (`design/spec-final.md:1859–1861`). A carried relic claims no ground, and the carrier must be able to walk home (`:542`). Relic victory requires controlling all relics in Monasteries (`:173`). These requirements coexist: a correctly functioning collector can deliver a known relic and correctly leave an unknown one, preventing any countdown.

The surrounding owner calculation independently confirms the victory contract. `src/game/simulation/bridge/matchEndOps.ts:126–164`, outside the reviewed feature hunks, returns no holding owner while any relic resource entity or carried reference remains, then requires one owner to hold the entire positive stored total. `src/game/simulation/bridge/systems/relicCountdownSystem.ts:40–60` clears clocks without that owner and creates a clock only when the condition holds. The search filters known ground at `src/game/simulation/bridge/monkAiSearchHelpers.ts:98–130`; deposition removes the resource and carrier entry and increments the own Monastery at `src/game/simulation/bridge/monkTaskAppliers.ts:435–453`. No product change is needed to make this particular absence of a countdown lawful.

### Accepted contract for the existing Standard match

Keep `aoe2-prototype`, both AI seats, Standard resources/difficulty, attacks enabled, all victory conditions, 100 ms simulation steps, the 45,000-tick horizon and the existing rendering-only optimization. Do not search for an easier seed, extend the horizon or change AI knowledge, planning, economy or movement to produce the old countdown trajectory. Preserve the original failure and ledger as provenance, and update the header to describe the actual new bounds.

The long case must still pass an unconditional positive mechanism check: observe at least one identified relic picked up by a live collector outside deposit range of its eventual own complete Monastery, observe that same carrier move while still holding that relic, then correlate removal of that resource/carry entry with an increment in the same owner's live complete Monastery. Independently noticing some movement and some store is insufficient. Do not accept a case with no pickup, no held movement or no delivery merely because its invariants hold.

Check conservation after each simulated tick: live relic resource entities plus valid stored counts equal the initial total. Carried relics already remain resource entities and must not be added a second time. Carried relic IDs must be unique and refer to live relic resources; carrier keys must resolve to live collectors. Stored-count keys must resolve to live complete Monasteries. Retain the old terminal empty carry-map requirement so one successful delivery cannot hide another stranded carrier.

A countdown must never exist before one owner holds all initial relics in its Monasteries. When that full holding condition is reached in the running Standard match, require the correct owner's countdown and no clock for another owner; do not merely skip all countdown assertions. If relics remain on the ground at the terminal bound, require them to be unexplored to the relevant live collectors through both their own and shared exploration, with at least one such collector present so this check is not vacuous. The current recorded match satisfies this premise. This is an explicit one-trajectory observation, not a promise that every future legitimate match must leave unknown relics or produce the same owner, delivery tick or age sequence.

Assert the loaded initial premise: all three relics exist on known ground, none is carried or stored, no relic countdown exists, the own Monastery is complete, the collector is live and the intended seat is under AI. From there, run the actual AI commands and simulation. Require every one of those three relic IDs to be picked up, carried through actual movement from outside deposit range, and deposited in its owner's complete Monastery. No direct pickup/deposit calls or prefilled relic counters may replace these actions.

This companion preserves and strengthens the positive all-relic outcome coverage even though the naturally developing Standard match no longer guarantees its strategic preconditions. Its short bound must be chosen and documented for these fixed routes and decision cadence, rather than repeatedly adjusted after failed executions. Countdown completion time, winner and all-map strategic scouting remain outside these tests' stated purpose.
